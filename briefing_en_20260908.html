SK hynix Backed by $27 Billion; Oil Prices Surge 8%

Foreign buying uplifts Korean chip stocks amid Middle East oil supply concerns.

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The KOSPI index rose 2.1% over the past week, driven by strength in semiconductor and financial sectors. SK hynix secured a government-backed investment of approximately 27 trillion KRW (around $200 billion), attracting significant foreign demand in Korea’s chip market. Meanwhile, U.S. Treasury yields increased by 0.12 percentage points, surpassing 4.5%, pressured by the surge in mega tech corporate bond issuance, which also bolstered the US dollar and created short-term volatility for emerging markets. Sector volatility widened globally due to uncertainties around bond issuance, Federal Reserve policy, and tech sector developments.

💡 Analyst Insights
The Korean semiconductor and IT heavyweights, alongside financial stocks, stand to benefit in the short term. SK hynix’s near-term price target is 200,000 KRW, with potential to rise to 240,000 KRW based on government support and revisited DRAM and HBM demand forecasts for next year. KB Financial Group is also poised to gain from increased corporate lending and fee income linked to semiconductor sector growth, as well as inflows of foreign capital. Globally, Nvidia is expected to profit from expanding AI infrastructure investments in the U.S. Recommended investment action is to increase exposure to Korean semiconductors (specifically SK hynix) and financials (KB Financial). However, caution is warranted as further spikes in U.S. Treasury yields could trigger foreign capital outflows and pullbacks in technology stocks.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and autonomous vehicle sectors are rapidly expanding both domestically and internationally. Global leaders such as Nvidia and Google are intensifying competition with advanced AI models like GPT-6 Astra and Gemini Flash 2.5, driving market optimism for AI-powered services including robotaxis. South Korean giants Samsung Electronics and Naver are accelerating efforts to strengthen generative AI capabilities and semiconductor competitiveness. The global AI market is projected to grow at over 20% annually, reaching around 500 trillion KRW (~$400 billion) by 2027.

Energy Energy · Industry

Rising geopolitical tensions between the U.S. and Iran have escalated instability in the Strait of Hormuz, pushing Brent crude oil prices up by 8.2% over the past week to exceed $86 per barrel. Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) announced expanded investment in Africa's largest refinery, signaling a shift in global crude supply chains. European initiatives to establish new state-owned renewable energy entities gain momentum, although near-term risks of natural gas and crude supply disruptions are growing. This uncertainty contributes to heightened price volatility among domestic and international energy firms.

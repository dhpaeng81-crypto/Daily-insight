Global Oil Nears $92 on Iran-US Clash; Energy and Defense Rally, IT Faces Short-Term Pullback

Rising Middle East tensions boost energy and defense sectors, while technology stocks see a temporary correction.

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Today, major global stock indices showed mixed performance. South Korea’s KOSPI gained 0.6% to reclaim the 2,780 level, driven by strength in energy and defense stocks amid heightened geopolitical risks. The US Nasdaq declined 0.8%, pressured by profit-taking in technology names, whereas the S&P 500 edged up 0.2% as growth names like Bloom Energy and Illumina contributed positive momentum. The US dollar weakened against the Korean won, with USD/KRW down 4 won to 1,352. Meanwhile, crude oil (WTI) climbed 0.28% to $91.22 per barrel, highlighting sector rotation and more differentiated market moves.

💡 Analyst Insights
The key beneficiaries today were energy and defense sectors, while the technology segment experienced a temporary pause. In South Korea, companies like Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering (KSOE) and Hanwha Aerospace outperformed, supported by concerns over Iranian-US military clashes and resultant oil transport disruptions. Internationally, Bloom Energy surged 8% following its inclusion in the S&P 500 and strong net-zero energy initiatives, alongside Illumina and Everpure benefiting from improved flows. We recommend increasing exposure to energy and defense stocks over the next 2–3 weeks, while adopting a cautious stance on large-cap tech names. Investors should be prepared for potential reversals if geopolitical tensions ease and US Treasury yields rise, triggering corrections in oil, defense, and a rebound in tech.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The IT sector faced a short-term downturn, led by AI-related stocks showing downward pressure. Leading AI semiconductor stocks dropped about 18% from their month-highs amid ongoing legal challenges in US AI journalism and expanding data center regulations, which temporarily muted momentum. Despite this, Korean suppliers such as SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics maintain robust medium-term outlooks, given their leading roles in AI DRAM and data center component markets. The global AI data center market is forecasted to grow at over 21% CAGR through 2027, reaching a scale exceeding 500 trillion KRW, indicating that current supply-demand headwinds are likely temporary.

Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices approached $92 per barrel today (WTI $91.22, +0.28%) amid escalating Iran-US military confrontations disrupting Persian Gulf shipping lanes. LNG futures rebounded 2.5% as security concerns heightened supply risk. Mutual retaliatory strikes between Iran and the US raised fears of prolonged disruptions in Middle Eastern oil and gas exports, driving a parallel rally in defense stocks alongside energy. Conversely, renewable energy equities softened slightly due to inflation concerns sparked by cascading oil price increases.

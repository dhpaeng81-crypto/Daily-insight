Oil Surpasses $90; South Korean Refiners Poised to Benefit

Rising crude and bond yields drive capital shift to Korean energy and financial stocks.

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

U.S. markets closed lower as oil prices surged past $90 per barrel, with the Dow Jones down 0.7%, S&P 500 falling 0.8%, and Nasdaq retreating 0.9%. The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield climbed to 4.32%, its highest in a month, pressuring growth shares amid heightened global tightening concerns. The Korean won weakened alongside these moves. Defensive sectors, particularly energy and financials, attracted increased investor interest amid growing uncertainty. Commodity inflation and bond yield rises weighed on investor appetite for technology and high-growth stocks.

💡 Analyst Insights
Short term, energy and financial sectors are outperforming while growth and tech equities see reduced allocations. In Korea, KB Financial Group stands out due to improved net interest margins and cost control, while S-Oil gains from higher refining margins and effective cost pass-through. Globally, ExxonMobil attracts attention for its resilience and profitability in oil and gas assets. The recommended strategy is to increase weighting in undervalued energy and financial stocks both for short and medium-term investment. However, investors should cautiously approach potential volatility triggered by oil price corrections, unexpected Fed rate hikes, or easing geopolitical tensions.

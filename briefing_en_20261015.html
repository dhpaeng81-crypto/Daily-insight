NVIDIA and Celltrion Rally as Oil Surges Over 4%

AI semiconductor and biotech lead gains amid sharp energy price spike from Middle East tensions

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The Korean KOSPI closed 1.2% higher at 2,750 points, while the KOSDAQ gained 1.5%. US tech-heavy NASDAQ rose 0.7%, with the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index up 1.3%, highlighting strength in AI and semiconductor-related sectors. Financials, secondary battery makers, and biotech stocks also showed notable gains, complemented by energy equipment and service providers benefiting from a 4.2% jump in crude oil prices. The US 10-year Treasury yield eased slightly to 4.45%, indicating a shift into risk assets.

💡 Analyst Insights
Sectors such as semiconductors, biotech, and energy are attracting capital reallocation. South Korea's Celltrion continues to benefit from a 25% year-over-year increase in biosimilar sales and expanding global market share. Samsung Electronics is seeing visible revenue growth with increased supply of high-margin memory chips driven by NVIDIA's escalating AI semiconductor demand. Globally, NVIDIA's stock has surged approximately 12% over the past two weeks as it solidifies leadership in AI chips. We recommend investors consider increasing short-term allocations to these segments, while remaining cautious about heightened volatility risks from US-China tensions, interest rate fluctuations, and geopolitical uncertainties.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI generation of music, semiconductor innovations, and data privacy issues are shaping market dynamics. NVIDIA and key semiconductor companies are advancing AI chip technology, with the sector forecasted to grow at a 35% annual pace, reaching a KRW 210 trillion (approximately USD 160 billion) market by 2026. Platforms like Google and YouTube Music are adjusting policies to address copyright and authenticity concerns amid the surge of AI-generated audio content. AI startups such as Apate and DeepSearch are emerging to meet increasing demands for security and authenticity verification, coinciding with a growing need for software and cloud security within Korea.

Energy Energy · Industry

Crude oil prices jumped 4.2% to USD 93 per barrel, accompanied by gas and electricity spot prices rising around 3%. Fears of supply disruptions intensified after unconfirmed reports of an attack on Saudi Aramco and growing instability in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical maritime logistics route. The European Union postponed its oil and gas import restrictions by one year, heightening market concerns over potential energy shortages this winter. US natural gas producers and pipeline operators face direct exposure to these external supply chain risks.

The decision impacts energy markets by maintaining current import standards temporarily, which may affect pricing and supply stability.

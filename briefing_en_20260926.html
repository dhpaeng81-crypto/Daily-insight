💡 Analyst Insights
Short-term capital inflow favors technology and AI-related sectors along with alternative assets like Bitcoin and gold. Korean companies Samsung Electronics and Naver stand to benefit from expanded AI semiconductor and cloud investments, representing key domestic growth exposures. Hanwha and Samsung Life are gaining buying momentum as perceived safe-haven plays amid uncertainty. Globally, Nvidia's ongoing AI chip investments and Akamai's cloud expansion position them as critical beneficiaries. We recommend overweighting AI, semiconductor, and alternative asset exposures in the short run, while monitoring risks from potential policy rate hikes or dollar volatility spikes that could trigger growth stock corrections or broader market volatility.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI chip market experienced over 25% growth in Q1, driven by new product launches and significant capital inflows. Global tech giants including Nvidia, Akamai, and Meta are committing multi-billion-dollar investments to expand data centers, cloud infrastructure, and AI platforms. Korean IT conglomerates are accelerating AI semiconductor R&D and cloud infrastructure expansion, generating positive spillovers across downstream industries such as equipment and components. This surge in AI computing demand marks a critical inflection point for revaluing companies with direct AI exposure.

Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices declined 3.4% to $77 per barrel following Saudi Arabia's 5%+ quarter-over-quarter export increase and progress in US-Iran nuclear negotiations. Renewable energy expansion and Latin American nuclear power projects are advancing, easing global energy supply pressures. However, geopolitical risks persist, including tensions around Middle Eastern oil transport routes and France's military involvement in Saudi Arabia's defense. These factors contribute to a cautiously softer, yet volatile, energy price environment.

Heating Oil Hits Record High, Energy Stocks Rally Amid Oil Surge

Oil prices surge 6% on Middle East tensions; SK Innovation, KEPCO gain; AI cloud investments boost tech stocks

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Major global indices showed mixed performances, ending largely flat. U.S. Treasury yields fell sharply by 0.14 percentage points, easing borrowing costs. Meanwhile, geopolitical risks in the Middle East propelled crude oil prices over 6% higher, pushing energy and financial stocks uplift. Conversely, some high-growth sectors paused for consolidation. Bitcoin surged nearly 5% amid concerns over dollar-denominated debt, approaching $80,000, as investors sought safe-haven dividend-paying and defensive-sector equities.

💡 Analyst Insights
Energy and dividend-focused sectors are presently benefiting from rising oil prices and declining bond yields. In South Korea, SK Innovation and Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) are well positioned to capitalize on higher crude and electricity tariff hikes, supported by government policy. Internationally, Chevron remains a key beneficiary of elevated oil prices. We recommend increasing energy sector exposure in the short term, with SK Innovation (target price KRW190,000) and KEPCO (target KRW27,000) as top picks due to strong pricing momentum. However, investors should carefully manage holdings, as relief in geopolitical tensions or rapid oil price corrections could result in short-term pullbacks. A global economic slowdown reducing physical demand also poses a material risk.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sectors continue to attract massive capital inflows, highlighted by multi-billion dollar cloud infrastructure deals. Anthropic committed approximately $11.6 billion over seven years to Akamai for data center cloud services, signaling robust demand for AI hardware and secure cloud hosting. Meta launched an early access program for its generative AI application, Muse, expanding user engagement. Concurrently, concerns around data security, confidentiality breaches, and environmental impacts of AI data centers have intensified, increasing demand for semiconductor and data security solutions domestically.

Energy Energy · Industry

International crude oil prices surpassed $90 per barrel, reaching a two-month high triggered by escalating Middle East geopolitical tensions. In the U.S., retail heating oil in Maine set an all-time high of $6.02 per gallon amid colder weather and supply concerns. The European Union delayed methane emission regulations, temporarily easing costs for energy importers. Seasonal demand and supply chain instabilities pushed power and gas prices up by 7-12%. Nuclear power and renewable energy policies are regaining emphasis globally as countries balance energy security and climate goals.

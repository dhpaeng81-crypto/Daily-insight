AI Theme Stocks Surge, Big Tech Earnings Crucial This Week

Korean IT and semiconductor sectors hit yearly highs amid AI commercialization growth

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

South Korea’s KOSPI index closed up 0.8% at the 2,700 level, supported by strong foreign buying particularly in IT, semiconductor, and secondary battery sectors. Defensive and domestic consumption stocks edged slightly lower. In the US, technology-heavy Nasdaq gained 1.2% ahead of major Big Tech earnings reports, reflecting robust investor appetite. The USD-KRW exchange rate remained stable around 1,380, indicating improved global risk sentiment. Overall, growth sectors drove market momentum while cautious positioning remained in more defensive areas.

💡 Analyst Insights
Semiconductors, AI, and ICT stocks stand out as key beneficiaries of current capital flows. Samsung Electronics is poised for further gains on the back of increased semiconductor equipment investments and recovering memory prices. Naver continues to expand its generative AI services, attracting foreign funds and boosting its competitive edge. Globally, Nvidia is expected to see its stock re-rated by over 15% due to rising AI infrastructure spending. Investors are advised to adopt a buy or accumulate stance on these growth names but remain vigilant for potential volatility triggered by earnings shocks from US Big Tech or a rise in South Korea’s benchmark interest rates. Portfolio diversification should be prioritized to avoid overconcentration in growth stocks.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

AI and generative AI firms are experiencing rapid user and revenue growth. Meta's AI app Muse now boasts over 10 million users, with shopping being a key monetization driver. US retail giants like Amazon and Walmart are expanding into AI-driven customer engagement, although approaches differ. Innovative startups such as Recursive, which recently released the AI self-improving coding model Flint, demonstrate accelerating AI development capabilities. These global trends are swiftly influencing South Korea’s ICT and semiconductor industries, underpinning expectations for a more than 15% investment increase in AI, cloud, and data centers in 2024.

💡 Analyst Insights
Among South Korean beneficiaries, SK Hynix is expanding supply of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) critical for AI workloads, while Kakao leverages AI in chatbots and ecommerce solutions. Meanwhile, Nvidia maintains its global leadership through dominant market share in AI chips and data center GPUs. Short-term strategies favor increasing exposure to SK Hynix and Kakao amid expanding AI demand, though risks from tighter technology regulations or disappointing results remain. Energy Energy · Industry

Following a more than 50% surge driven by conflict risks in Iran, crude oil prices have softened to the mid-$80s per barrel due to US strategic heating oil releases. The US Energy Department announced additional heating oil supplies ahead of winter to stabilize the market. In Europe, debates continue around new oilfield approvals amid rising electricity costs and supply uncertainties. South Korea’s energy import prices have fluctuated slightly but are balanced by stable oil prices and government policy buffers. Overall, the energy market faces short-term easing with medium-term geopolitical uncertainties.

Volatility in energy prices may temporarily pressure refinery companies such as SK Innovation and GS Caltex. Conversely, utilities focused on heating and power like Daesung Energy and Korea Electric Power Corporation could benefit from greater supply stability and reduced costs due to US heating oil reserve releases. However, investors should stay alert to potential oil price shocks triggered by renewed Middle East geopolitical tensions.

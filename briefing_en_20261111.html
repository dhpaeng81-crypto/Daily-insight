Today's Briefing

OpenAI and Anthropic Spotlighted Amid Regulatory and IPO Developments

AI sector volatility intensifies on regulatory moves and major acquisitions in generative AI.

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The KOSPI index closed up 0.6% at 2,824 points, supported by pronounced foreign net buying. Financials and information technology sectors led gains, rising 1.2% and 1.7%, respectively, driving the broader market up. The US Federal Reserve held interest rates steady at 5.25-5.50%, while expectations persist for a possible 0.25% rate cut in the second half of the year, boosting investor sentiment. Meanwhile, tech-heavy US indices rebounded strongly with the Nasdaq up 1.1% and the S&P 500 rising 0.9%, reflecting resilience in AI and energy demand amid global economic slowdown concerns.

💡 Analyst Insights
Expanding exposure to information technology and holding companies remains prudent. Samsung Electronics is positioned for upside with a forecasted 15%+ rise in DRAM prices in H2 and stronger foundry orders. Kakao benefits from expanding digital ecosystem services, favoring both short-term and medium-term gains. Globally, Microsoft stands out as a direct beneficiary of generative AI growth and robust Azure cloud adoption. Investment actions include selective net buying in IT sectors and maintaining a hold on interest rate-sensitive or consumer stocks due to geopolitical and rate policy uncertainties. Investors should also prepare for potential market corrections should rate cuts be delayed or geopolitical tensions escalate, particularly related to Ukraine or Middle East developments.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Significant AI sector events include OpenAI's acquisition of travel startup Odessia and Anthropic’s move to go public via an IPO with founders cashing out $250 million each. New York State enacted comprehensive AI safety legislation, mandating registration and safety disclosures from major players like OpenAI and Anthropic. Google and Adobe announced integrations of generative AI into productivity software, underscoring AI’s expanding application. The Korean AI market is anticipated to grow annually by 19% (2024-2027) from 10 trillion to 17 trillion KRW, fueling short-term gains in related stocks amid heightened volatility from parallel innovation and regulatory tightening.

💡 Analyst Insights
Short-term buying opportunities are evident in Korean cloud and semiconductor providers, with Naver enhancing cloud services and hybrid AI collaborations, and SK Hynix expanding high-bandwidth memory (HBM) supply, tied to Nvidia sales. Microsoft remains a dominant global leader benefiting from Azure-based generative AI market leadership. Recommended actions are to increase positions in AI and semiconductor shares in the short term while diversifying portfolios to manage medium-to-long-term regulatory risks and evolving large-cap corporate convergence. Investors should remain cautious about potential growth constraints from increasing AI safety regulations domestically and internationally.
Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices stabilized around $82 per barrel after a 4.1% rise over two weeks, halting earlier declines. Natural gas prices rebounded modestly amid discussions on Arctic production resumption. Electricity demand is rising due to AI data center expansion and renewable energy transitions, with global power consumption expected to increase by 4% by 2025 per IEA forecasts. Key geopolitical dynamics include Arctic gas field developments in North America and energy investment debates in Europe, complicated by renewed COP31 challenges over achieving a 35% electricity share target by 2035 amid conflicting fossil fuel and renewable interests.

Countries fight over keeping electricity pledge clean at COP31 - Financial Times
At COP31, countries are debating a critical proposal to increase electricity's share of global energy consumption to 35% by 2035. However, there is concern among some negotiators that the pledge could be compromised by including electricity generated from fossil fuels, undermining its environmental goals. This dispute highlights the challenges in balancing energy transition ambitions with economic and political realities. Investors should watch how the final agreement shapes renewable energy policies, as it will impact clean energy markets and related investment opportunities.

💡 Analyst Insights
The sharp rise in petroleum product prices is providing near-term benefits to refining, transportation, and energy storage sectors. In Korea, leading refiners S-OIL and Daehan Petrochem are expected to enjoy margin expansion driven by elevated oil and diesel prices, triggering positive stock momentum. Internationally, ExxonMobil continues to demonstrate strong share performance amid the energy price rally. We recommend increasing exposure to S-OIL and Daehan Petrochem for short-term gains, with potential for further upside should oil prices exceed current highs. However, investors should remain vigilant to risks such as unexpected Federal Reserve tightening and escalating geopolitical tensions, particularly between the U.S. and Iran, which could trigger rapid market corrections.

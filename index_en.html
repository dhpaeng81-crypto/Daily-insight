Energy Energy · Industry

International crude oil prices edged up 1.2%, reaching $82.3 per barrel amid ongoing geopolitical tensions. The European Union achieved a historic milestone by sourcing 31% of its electricity from renewable energies such as solar and wind, underscoring a major energy transition. Global banks increased their energy-sector financing to KRW 2,300 trillion by 2025 projections, marking a 13% rise over the past year. However, ongoing US sanctions related to Russia’s oil exports to India and geopolitical risks in the Middle East, including conflict in Yemen, continue to threaten supply stability and contribute to price volatility.

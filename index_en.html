Fed Rate Decision D-Day and New AI Features Unveiled

Tech stocks retreat amid Fed rate decision anticipation and AI innovation acceleration

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US equities fluctuated ahead of the Federal Reserve’s interest rate decision scheduled early Korean time on the 15th. The S&P 500 closed down 0.5%, while the Nasdaq dropped 0.8%, extending a two-day selloff particularly concentrated in financial stocks. A mysterious trader took a 163 billion KRW options position just before the Fed announcement, signaling heightened market volatility expectations. Defensive sectors like real estate and utilities saw increased weighting, while emerging markets traded sideways influenced by currency fluctuations. The Korean won ended slightly firmer around 1,375 per USD.

💡 Analyst Insights
In a scenario where the Fed holds rates steady, growth-sensitive sectors such as technology and domestic consumption in Korea are poised for upside. Conversely, an interest rate hike would favor bank stocks and defensive sectors. Korean financial companies KB Financial Group and Shinhan Financial Group are recommended for short-term buying due to stable dividends and margin preservation. For international exposure, Wells Fargo stands out as a beneficiary of US rate hikes. We advise maintaining a cautious watch (‘hold’), considering a tactical shift to defensive assets such as Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) and SK Telecom if the Fed holds rates. Key risks include aggressive Fed tightening beyond expectations, a sharp rise in bond yields, and capital outflows from emerging markets.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Leading AI firms Anthropic and OpenAI announced plans to deploy continuous safety evaluators, reinforcing risk management frameworks amid growing scrutiny. Google introduced AI agents capable of direct smart home device controls, exemplifying rapid AI application expansion. This development boosts medium-term growth prospects for Korean industries focused on AI infrastructure, particularly data centers and semiconductors. The global AI market is forecasted to grow at an 18% CAGR from 2024 to 2027, reaching a market size of approximately 240 trillion KRW, with major technology giants like Meta and NVIDIA increasing their technological dependencies.

Energy Energy · Industry

International crude oil prices rose 0.7% to $82 per barrel, supported by declining US inventories and the possibility of extended Saudi production cuts. Renewable energy sectors in Europe and the US experienced high volatility amid policy uncertainties. Domestically, expectations of a 2-4% electricity rate hike due to the summer peak have triggered a sectoral rebound in related stocks. Spot natural gas prices also registered a 1.1% short-term recovery, reflecting supply concerns.

💡 Analyst Insights
The oil price increase pressures downstream sectors such as petrochemicals, impacting Korean companies like Lotte Chemical and Hanwha Solutions through rising input costs. Conversely, integrated refiners SK Innovation and S-OIL are expected to improve short-term profitability through higher refining margins. US major ExxonMobil benefits from production cuts alongside increased shale output, enhancing earnings outlook. We recommend a ‘buy’ rating on SK Innovation and S-OIL while trimming exposure to chemical sector stocks. Geopolitical risks, especially Middle East supply disruptions, combined with a potential US economic slowdown, stress the importance of holding defensive assets and portfolio diversification.

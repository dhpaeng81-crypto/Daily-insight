Oil Price Surge & AI Infrastructure Investments Drive Sector Divergence

Rising oil prices and AI capex boost energy rebound and IT growth in Korea and globally

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Goldman Sachs has issued a warning on further upward pressure on international oil prices, reviving energy sector risks. Victoria's Secret shares surged nearly 300%, driven by rising demand for GLP-1 weight-loss drugs, creating a rare rally in healthcare and consumer discretionary stocks. Major U.S. indices mostly traded sideways, with healthcare and AI infrastructure-related names outperforming. Stable bond demand showed slight softening. Global investors are reallocating toward growth stocks less sensitive to economic cycles, especially AI infrastructure players.

💡 Analyst Insights
The warning on oil price increases raises the probability of a near-term rebound for the energy and refining sectors. In Korea, S-Oil and SK Innovation stand out as beneficiaries, with expected earnings improvements fueled by rising crude prices and refining margins. Globally, ExxonMobil is positioned to gain from the current energy price uptrend due to its cost controls. We recommend a buy strategy on S-Oil and SK Innovation for short-term gains. Healthcare and consumer sectors could see renewed interest, with companies like Dong-A ST and Shinsegae International gaining from expanding GLP-1 demand. However, a risk remains if geopolitical tensions ease and oil prices fall sharply, potentially increasing market volatility.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The U.S. Department of Defense has integrated major AI tools including ChatGPT and Grok, accelerating AI adoption across military and public sectors. Nvidia’s $3.5 billion investment in Taiwan’s MediaTek underscores intensified competition to dominate global AI infrastructure markets. Korea’s data center, semiconductor, and cybersecurity sectors are set to grow annually by over 10% through 2024-2025. This trend supports Korean IT heavyweights as well as small and medium enterprises in cloud and semiconductor materials industries.

Energy Energy · Industry

U.S. natural gas and electricity prices remain elevated, driven by heatwaves and abnormal weather patterns. Renewable energy developments, notably Oregon State University’s wave energy facility startup, indicate accelerating diversification in energy sources. The energy market is increasingly characterized by competition between traditional fossil fuels and expanding renewable alternatives.

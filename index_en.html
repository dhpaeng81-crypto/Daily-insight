Meta’s Muse AI App Surpasses 10 Million Users Amid Surge in AI Industry Growth

Rapid AI app adoption and rising energy prices spark sector-wide ripple effects

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global markets edged higher as the US Federal Reserve maintained its benchmark interest rate, promoting modest investor optimism. The South Korean Kospi index rose 1.2%, closing around 2,720 points, led by robust performance in semiconductor and secondary battery sectors. Cyclical stocks gained from easing global inflation expectations, while energy and chemical stocks showed volatility due to oil price instability. The US 10-year Treasury yield slightly decreased to 4.44%, reflecting renewed stability in bond markets.

💡 Analyst Insights
Currently, sectors such as AI, big tech, and secondary batteries are exhibiting strong momentum. Samsung Electronics is benefiting from increased orders in metaverse and AI server components, while LG Energy Solution’s electric vehicle battery demand continues to accelerate, both expecting upward earnings revisions this year. Globally, Nvidia stands out with projections for nearly 150% year-over-year revenue growth in Q2 2024, driven by soaring AI semiconductor demand. We recommend an overweight stance on Korean technology stocks in the near term. However, investors should be cautious of potential volatility from prolonged US high interest rate environments impacting growth stocks.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The global AI and IT industries are experiencing rapid expansion driven by surging user adoption and commercialization of new technologies. Meta announced its AI application 'Muse' has surpassed 10 million users, highlighting AI's deep penetration into productivity and e-commerce activities. Startups like Recursive have introduced innovative self-evolving 'AI incubator' models, while retailers such as Walmart have deployed AI agents for automated purchasing, contrasting with Amazon’s restrictive approach. Korean firms are accelerating development and commercialization efforts in AI applications, autonomous driving AI, and productivity software.

💡 Analyst Insights
The key beneficiaries in AI/IT include semiconductor makers, data centers, cloud service providers, and e-commerce platforms. SK Hynix is expanding supplies of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) critical for AI servers, and Naver is diversifying revenue through generative AI offerings and enhanced cloud services. Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices surged about 15% over the past month, with WTI crude oil breaking above $86 per barrel. The US plans to release heating oil reserves ahead of midterm elections to ease domestic supply pressures. European and US natural gas prices remain highly volatile, fluctuating near €27 per MWh and $2.9 per 1000 cubic feet respectively. Environmental challenges and logistical disruptions in producer nations like Indonesia are exacerbating supply uncertainties for palm oil, crude oil, and LNG shipments. Rising oil costs are pressuring manufacturing margins and export competitiveness broadly.

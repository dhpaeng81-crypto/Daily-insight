Finance Financial Markets

Major markets like the U.S. hold steady with slight volatility amid fading rate cut expectations and growth concerns. Energy Energy · Industry

Crude oil prices softened, with WTI falling 2.1% to $78.20 per barrel amid supply disruptions through the Strait of Hormuz but waning demand forecasts from the International Energy Agency and rising inventories. Natural gas prices declined an additional 3.2% over the past week, though price surge risks are emerging in southern U.S. regions. Major producers like Venezuela and the U.S. face supply uncertainties from reduced investments and operational setbacks. Renewable energy growth pressures cost-sensitive sectors including chemicals, aviation, and logistics.

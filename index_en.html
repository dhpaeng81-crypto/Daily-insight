AI Panic Wipes Out ₩2,300 Trillion in Korean Semiconductor Market Cap

AI bubble fears trigger semiconductor sell-off and sharp decline in investor sentiment

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global equity markets experienced sharp declines led by semiconductor stocks. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index plummeted 10.7%, erasing approximately $1.1 trillion in market capitalization in one day. Growth and technology sectors broadly suffered sell-offs, while defensive sectors such as consumer staples and utilities remained relatively resilient. Despite expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hold, major government bond yields declined, indicating renewed risk-off sentiment and increased demand for safe-haven assets.

💡 Analyst Insights
The sudden collapse in AI-driven growth stocks, particularly semiconductors, has shifted investor preference towards stable earnings sectors like banking and energy. In Korea, despite Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix expanding their AI semiconductor businesses, short-term sentiment has sharply cooled, prompting a recommendation for medium to short-term investors to reduce exposure or maintain a cautious stance. Globally, Nvidia also saw over a 10% drop after strong Q1 results, highlighting the need for vigilant risk management amid ongoing volatility. Foreign capital inflows are expected to temporarily favor Korean banks such as Shinhan Financial Group and energy firms including Korea Gas Corporation. However, prolonged downturn risks remain due to potential Federal Reserve policy shifts and geopolitical tensions surrounding US-China AI competition and regulatory tightening.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Concerns of an overheated AI market have materialized, leading to significant corrections among key semiconductor players. Over the past week, semiconductor market capitalization contracted by roughly ₩2,300 trillion (approximately $1.8 trillion), signifying a cooling of speculative excesses. While sub-themes like AI platforms, cybersecurity, and data center efficiency continue to garner investor interest, overall market sentiment has softened. International AI leaders such as Nvidia and Microsoft maintain strong growth trajectories despite heightened short-term volatility in their share prices, reflecting increased uncertainty in the AI sector's near-term outlook.

💡 Analyst Insights
Within Korea, sectors linked to cybersecurity (AhnLab, Igloo Corporation) and IT infrastructure (Naver Cloud, SK Broadband) remain relatively attractive due to growing demand for transparency, security, and efficiency in AI applications. Global leader Nvidia continues to represent a compelling long-term story, though a prudent 'hold' stance is recommended amid current market turbulence. Domestic battery manufacturers like L&F and EcoPro BM, together with semiconductor equipment suppliers such as Wonik IPS and Hanwha Systems, benefit from cascading demand driven by AI-related technology upgrades, warranting a selective phased buying approach. Major risks include intensified government regulations on AI, escalating US-China tech rivalry, and potential disappointments in AI monetization outcomes.
Energy Energy · Industry

The global energy landscape is evolving with parallel developments in oil sands, offshore fields, and next-generation power technologies including nuclear fusion and geothermal energy. Notably, Canada’s Bay du Nord offshore oil project has cleared significant regulatory hurdles, supporting expectations of increased North American oil production in the medium to long term. Concurrently, investments in ultra-high temperature geothermal and hydrogen energy are rising, especially as solutions to data center power shortages. Korea Gas Corporation is positioned to benefit from stabilized international gas prices, improving its performance prospects for the second half of the year.

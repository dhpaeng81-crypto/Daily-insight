Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices fell sharply due to easing geopolitical risks, including Iran’s conditional peace proposals to the US, pushing Brent crude down 3.2% to $81.1 per barrel. Prices for related energy commodities such as natural gas and coal also declined, alleviating cost pressures on resource-intensive Korean companies like Hanwha Energy and POSCO. Developed markets continue to promote electric vehicle adoption, with California’s EV incentives expected to help EV market penetration surpass 18% in 2024. Consequently, renewable energy investment is accelerating, benefitting major energy corporations in Japan and the US through increased order books and earnings.

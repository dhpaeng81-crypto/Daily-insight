AI & Data Center Demand Surge Boosts Energy and IT Stocks

AI-driven power demand fuels gains in Korean traditional and IT sectors amid ongoing risks

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Despite concerns over slowing global economic momentum, major indices showed moderate gains today. The KOSPI advanced 0.4% to reclaim the 2,760 level, while U.S. Dow Jones futures indicated an early 0.3% rise. Weakness in the Japanese yen and cautious U.S. interest rate sentiment triggered short-term market adjustments. Nonetheless, large-cap IT and energy stocks led the uptrend, supported by a rotation into financial, energy, and IT sectors. Capital inflows were notably strong in export-driven manufacturing and data center-related industries.

💡 Analyst Insights
Financial markets are currently benefiting from a dual surge in AI/data center-related equities alongside traditional energy and manufacturing exporters. In Korea, SK Hynix is prominently highlighted for its strong memory sales driven by data center demand, while LS ELECTRIC benefits from growth in power supply equipment. Globally, NVIDIA stands out as a key beneficiary due to its AI semiconductor leadership. Investors should consider maintaining or increasing exposure to AI, energy infrastructure, and export manufacturing stocks in the near term. Key risks include potential abrupt shifts in U.S. interest rates, currency policy moves in Japan, and a correction in the AI bubble.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sector rallied significantly on news of surging demand for AI and data center technologies. The global AI and data center market is expanding at over 20% annual growth, expected to reach $300 billion by 2025. Korean tech giants Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are gaining competitive advantages through their memory and high-performance semiconductor offerings. The weak Korean won and strong performances of U.S. and Japanese tech stocks have had a positive spillover effect on Korean IT equities.

The rapid expansion of AI-powered data centers is materially benefitting Korean electronics components, semiconductor, and power solution industries. SK Hynix leads with its high-performance DRAM and HBM products, while Samsung Electronics excels in server memory and foundry capabilities. Energy Energy · Industry

Energy markets are under upward pressure from rising electricity and gas demand triggered by AI data centers and industrial power needs. As of July, Brent crude oil reached $83 per barrel, marking a 6% increase over the previous month; natural gas prices also rose by over 3%. While renewable energy investments are growing, AI and manufacturing facilities continue to rely heavily on fossil fuel-derived power. This dynamic is expected to sustain upward pressure on industrial electricity tariffs over the medium to long term.

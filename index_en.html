AI and Energy Deregulation Boost Semiconductor and Power Stocks

AI investment pullback and energy permit acceleration drive Korean sector rallies

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The South Korean KOSPI index rose 0.3% to 2,700, led by strength in technology and electric power sectors. In the United States, the S&P 500 saw a 0.5% gain in energy and semiconductor stocks despite mixed tech performance, while the Nasdaq fell 0.2%. Over the past five months, the global AI sector has corrected sharply, dropping over 50%, triggering rotation toward other growth stocks. The 10-year US Treasury yield edged down to 4.26%, reflecting easing expectations for interest rate hikes. The USD/KRW exchange rate remained stable around 1,370.

💡 Analyst Insights
Semiconductors and power and energy infrastructure sectors remain near-term and medium-term beneficiaries. South Korean companies SK Hynix, benefiting from persistent demand for memory semiconductors and AI chips, and Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO), poised for higher power supply and tariff normalization, are leading domestic plays. Globally, Nvidia has shown resilient earnings despite CEO-related controversies, supporting its rebound after recent correction. The recommended position is overweight, approaching entry after the significant AI sector decline. Key risks include the upcoming US presidential election, AI demand slowdown, and regulatory uncertainties such as potential US restrictions on AI technology exports.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT markets are experiencing short-term volatility following the CEO turmoil at Nvidia and a substantial sector correction exceeding 50%. Former US President Trump's announcement to penalize US firms building AI data centers abroad is increasing supply chain uncertainty for multinational IT companies. Nonetheless, AI applications continue to drive industrial expansion and efficiency gains, accelerating adoption in areas like AI-assisted coding and smart office technologies. Domestically, Korea's semiconductor and data center industries are expected to increase local capital expenditures amid concerns over global supply chain disruptions. The global AI market is projected to grow at an annual rate exceeding 17% through 2024.

Energy Energy · Industry

WTI crude oil prices plunged 5% in one day to $77.5 per barrel amid rising concerns over excess supply. The US Senate passed legislation to streamline permitting and approvals for energy projects, signaling expanded natural gas and renewable energy capacity. Lodestone Energy reported a 57% production increase and 36% sales growth in the first quarter but noted worsening profitability. These developments favorably impact global energy supply, improving conditions in electricity markets, with positive spillover effects anticipated for Korean power generation and transmission companies.

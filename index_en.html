Nvidia Surges 14%, Nasdaq Jumps Over 7% on AI Sentiment Recovery

Tech stocks rally as Nvidia’s CEO returns, easing scandal fears and boosting AI sector

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The Nasdaq Composite index surged more than 7% following a 14% one-day jump in Nvidia shares after Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO resolved recent scandal concerns. The technology sector, particularly AI-focused firms, led gains with semiconductor ETFs rallying 9% over three consecutive trading days. Meanwhile, Korea’s KOSPI index climbed 1.8%, driven by renewed foreign inflows into large-cap stocks like Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. The US dollar weakened by about 1%, complemented by a slight rise in US Treasury yields, reflecting strengthened risk appetite among investors.

💡 Analyst Insights
AI semiconductor, electric vehicle, and cloud sectors are experiencing strong short-term buying momentum. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are benefiting from supply chain optimism linked to Nvidia, with both expected to post over 30% year-on-year operating profit growth in Q2. Nvidia’s 14% rebound post-reinstatement re-establishes it as a market leader in AI chips globally. Investors should consider increasing exposure (‘overweight’) to these sectors for near-term gains but remain cautious about potential scandal re-emergence and escalating US-China semiconductor regulatory risks which could negatively affect margins and supply chains.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Global AI and IT market sentiment has rebounded substantially following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s return amid prior controversy. Google continues to expand AI-powered personalized search by integrating user data from Google Drive and Wallet, while Amazon announced a $7.5 billion acquisition to enhance its AI chip software capabilities, intensifying Big Tech’s competitive landscape. The worldwide AI market is forecasted to grow 25% this year, reaching approximately KRW 1,500 trillion (~USD 1.2 trillion), creating significant tailwinds for Korea’s internet, semiconductor, and data center industries.

Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices rebounded nearly 2% amid concerns over potential terrorist threats to Saudi Arabian refineries and increasing US electricity demand, with WTI crude nearing USD 81 per barrel. Energy costs are under upward pressure in key markets like the US, UK, and Michigan due to rising electricity and gas rates alongside growth in infrastructure investments. Europe experiences stronger renewable energy sector activity driven by increased grid investments and worsening water resource constraints. Overall, geopolitical tensions and climate change implications are heightening energy price volatility.

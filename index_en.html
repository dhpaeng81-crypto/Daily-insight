AI Stocks Surge 10% Led by Nvidia's AI PC Launch

Korean semiconductor and AI blue chips rally; short-term buy opportunity on Nvidia-Korea synergy

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The KOSPI index rebounded 0.7% today, reclaiming the 2,750 level. Gains were driven primarily by semiconductor shares such as Samsung Electronics (+1.8%) and SK Hynix (+4.2%), alongside secondary battery leader Samsung SDI (+3.3%). The US Philadelphia Semiconductor Index posted a 3% recovery yesterday, powered by Nvidia's 7% jump that revived investor sentiment. Meanwhile, the US 10-year Treasury yield eased slightly to 4.44%, and the USD/KRW exchange rate stabilized in the 1,370 range.

💡 Analyst Insights
Semiconductor and AI-related stocks were the clear beneficiaries of today's market upturn. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix stand to gain from their partnership with Nvidia and the surging demand for AI server memory, with expected annual earnings growth of around 20%. Globally, Nvidia's expansion into AI PCs and new server contracts have elevated it to the world's 2nd largest market cap at approximately KRW 3,200 trillion, attracting international IT capital. We recommend short-term accumulation of Korean semiconductor and AI supply chain stocks, while adopting a wait-and-see approach on secondary battery equities. Risks include lingering US Fed rate uncertainty ahead of the presidential election, which could induce short-term volatility or corrections.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The global AI and IT sector market capitalization rose 8% over the last week, paralleled by a 5% gain in the KOSPI IT sector. Microsoft launched AI PCs powered by Nvidia chips, updating Windows 11 to enhance AI model performance and agent operations. OpenAI also debuted a new visual interface for ChatGPT, intensifying the hardware-software integration competition. Korean giants Samsung Electronics and LG Electronics are expanding their AI PC lineups, benefiting also Taiwan's memory and GPU supply chains. The global AI semiconductor market is projected to grow from KRW 20 trillion this year to KRW 30 trillion next year.

💡 Analyst Insights
The proliferation of AI PCs and visual AI platforms is set to boost earnings for Korean companies specializing in semiconductors (e.g., Samsung Electronics' HBM memory), AI PC manufacturing (Samsung Electronics, LG Electronics), and software services (Naver, Kakao Enterprise). Samsung and SK Hynix occupy critical roles in the AI ecosystem by expanding high-bandwidth memory production and AI server supply. Nvidia remains the global AI PC ecosystem leader, further strengthened by its collaboration with Microsoft. We recommend a buy stance on AI and semiconductor stocks for the near term, while monitoring intensifying competition among smaller AI software developers and price volatility. Potential risk triggers include tightened US/EU regulatory frameworks, delays in key new product launches from Nvidia or Samsung, and a sharp decline in memory prices.
Energy Energy · Industry

International crude oil prices declined 3.5% over the past week, with West Texas Intermediate trading near $78 per barrel. The US government announced additional strategic petroleum reserve releases along with looming new regulatory measures on oil and gas operations, dampening investment sentiment. Qatar's liquefied natural gas (LNG) expansion, adding 12 million tons annually, is expected to begin operations in November, putting downward pressure on gas prices. While Northeast Asia anticipates winter heating demand, concerns persist over secure fuel supply, limiting prospects for improved profitability among South Korean refiners and gas companies.

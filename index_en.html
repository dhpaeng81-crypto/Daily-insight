Canva & OpenAI Accelerate AI Commercialization, Samsung & Naver Poised to Benefit

AI-driven decision and generative technologies spark IT platform and semiconductor resurgence

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The Korean financial market is rebounding amid volatility, led by large IT stocks. The KOSPI rose 0.8% to 2,695 points, while the Nasdaq gained 1.2%, closing near 15,850 points. Conversely, financials and traditional manufacturing sectors underperformed amid lingering uncertainties. Global capital continues to prioritize AI and high-tech IT sectors into Q2. The USD/KRW exchange rate stabilized around 1,370 won, supported by optimism over recovery in advanced semiconductor demand.

💡 Analyst Insights
IT platforms, semiconductors, and data centers remain the strongest beneficiaries in both short and medium term. Domestically, Samsung Electronics is expected to benefit from memory and system semiconductor price rebounds, while Naver's cloud and AI platform businesses show robust growth potential. Globally, Microsoft maintains strong momentum through expanding cloud and generative AI services. We recommend an overweight stance on Samsung Electronics and Naver for both short- and mid-term horizons. However, concentrated capital inflow into AI momentum, U.S. policy risks including interest rates and regulatory developments, and intensifying competition pose downside risks.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

AI technologies for answering, decision-making, and image generation are entering commercial deployment. OpenAI's launch of a new decision model and Canva's debut image generation model are driving cost reductions and enabling tailored services. Microsoft disclosed that its AI diagnostic system outperforms frontline doctors on complex medical cases, accelerating innovation in healthcare AI. Korean IT platforms are rapidly advancing generative AI capabilities. Market research predicts the cloud segment based on AI and generative technologies to grow annually by over 15% through 2025. Meanwhile, cybersecurity threats such as hacking and data breaches are escalating in parallel with accelerated AI adoption in finance and security sectors.

💡 Analyst Insights
Korean AI platform, semiconductor, and cloud companies are positioned as top beneficiaries in the near term. Naver is forecasted to achieve over 15% revenue growth in H2 2024 due to enhanced generative AI technologies and cloud platform expansion. Samsung Electronics is expected to surpass 3 trillion won in semiconductor operating profit in Q3, driven by surging demand for high-performance DRAM and high-bandwidth memory (HBM). On the global stage, Microsoft is solidifying its top position through cloud and AI platform expansion. We advise increasing exposure to Naver and Samsung Electronics given high mid-term profit potential. However, intense competition among OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft might slow down commercialization speed despite technological advances.
Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices, as measured by WTI, have declined 2.3% over the past week to the $79 per barrel range, with power and gas prices also stabilizing slightly. Persian Gulf oil exports reached an all-time high of 24 million barrels per day, offsetting geopolitical tensions related to Iran. The U.S. Department of Energy announced a $125 million (1.7 trillion KRW) support package to enhance electricity access for residential and power generation sectors. Meanwhile, skepticism over Venezuela’s reported 300 billion barrels of oil reserves is rising amid questions on the accuracy of their estimations. The domestic electricity market remains neutral short-term, but renewables and energy infrastructure investments present long-term upside.

💡 Analyst Insights
Stable oil and electricity prices support near-term profitability improvements for energy-intensive industries such as manufacturing, chemicals, and aviation. In Korea, Korean Air benefits from reduced fuel cost pressures, while Hanwha Solutions sees improved solar segment margins due to cost stabilization. Globally, Chevron stands to gain from merger synergies and normalization of logistics. Overweight positions in these beneficiaries are prudent short term, complemented by diversified strategies to hedge potential energy price rebounds. Downside risks include escalating Persian Gulf tensions and demand weakness tied to U.S. economic slowdown.

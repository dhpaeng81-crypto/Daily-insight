The rapid drop in crude oil prices has boosted profit outlooks for energy-intensive sectors such as aviation, shipping, and chemicals. Korean companies like Korean Air are expected to see quarterly operating profits increase by over 10% due to reduced jet fuel expenses, while LG Chem benefits from lower raw material costs improving margins. We recommend a short-term 'buy' stance focusing on energy-consuming industries with a view to increasing portfolio exposure. Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sectors are experiencing rapid growth driven by surging investments in data centers and AI infrastructure. Worldwide data center electricity demand is rising by over 20% annually, fueling expansion in data center cooling systems and AI semiconductor markets, which are projected to exceed $100 billion by 2026. South Korean conglomerates are planning data center infrastructure investments around 10 trillion KRW (~$7.5 billion) through 2024-2025, with strong growth in security and AI software firms. This expansion highlights emerging social challenges around AI ethics, cybersecurity, and power supply bottlenecks.

SpaceX Reports $4.4B Q3 Profit, AI Drives Growth Momentum

AI-focused firms lead gains; cloud and semiconductor stocks show short-term strength

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US stock markets advanced on robust earnings from major tech players, with the S&P 500 rising 0.8% and the Nasdaq gaining 1.2%. The AI, semiconductor, and cloud sectors outperformed, each climbing around 2%. Japanese and European markets followed suit, rising approximately 0.5%. However, US 10-year Treasury yields increased by 0.18 percentage points to 4.45%, reflecting persistent expectations of Federal Reserve rate hikes. The Korean won stabilized near 1,370 per US dollar, supporting foreign buying momentum in Korean equities.

💡 Analyst Insights
AI, semiconductor, and cloud infrastructure sectors remain key beneficiaries of current market trends. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are positioned for upward earnings revisions in Q4 due to rising demand for AI servers and memory chips. Naver is expected to see a pickup in its share price backed by expansive AI infrastructure investments and strong cloud service growth. Globally, NVIDIA maintains its dominance with over 60% revenue growth this year, supported by its exclusive leadership in AI compute semiconductors. We recommend increasing allocations to AI, semiconductor, and cloud-related equities in the short term. Caution is warranted for potential growth stock corrections driven by prolonged US interest rate hikes and rising bond yields.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI sector experiences increased volatility amid the launch of large-scale projects and emergent regulatory scrutiny. SpaceX leveraged AI integration to deliver a stunning $4.4 billion in Q3 net profit, surpassing expectations. OpenAI is diversifying into biotech AI through its acquisition of Orchestra Bio, enhancing its life science AI portfolio. High-performance AI startups like Anthropic grapple with compute resource shortages amid cloud provider competition and regulatory uncertainties. The US government’s tightening of AI security policies, including restrictions on Chinese AI technologies, underscores geopolitical and operational risks. Domestic Korean AI and cloud investments are accelerating, fueling growth in software and data center industries.

💡 Analyst Insights
Rising demand for AI and cloud computing directly benefits Korean data center operators, semiconductor manufacturers, and software firms. Naver’s cloud and data center expansion alongside AI-driven service enhancements highlight its growth trajectory. Kakao is emerging as a notable beneficiary with its focus on generative AI and enterprise chatbot solutions. Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices edged higher given uncertainties surrounding new North Sea oil field approvals and incidents at US oilfields. West Texas Intermediate crude settled at $80.5 per barrel, up 1.3% from the previous day. Natural gas and power stocks gained approximately 2%, buoyed by projections of increased AI-driven energy demand. China's expansion of electricity and gas supply to robotics and AI sectors intensifies energy competition across Asia. The energy crisis’s ripple effects into food security and other industries keep investor sentiment sensitive and reactive in the short term.

💡 Analyst Insights
Rising oil and gas prices pose cost pressures on energy-intensive sectors such as chemicals and transportation. Nonetheless, companies like Hanwha Solutions, specializing in solar energy and energy storage, and S-Oil, benefiting from refining margin improvements, are expected to report earnings gains. Conversely, firms with high energy consumption like Hyundai Steel and Korean Air may face profitability challenges. Internationally, ExxonMobil is capitalizing on the rebound in crude and gas prices. We recommend a tactical increase in oil and gas-related stocks alongside a strategic overweight in solar and energy storage sectors. Market participants must remain vigilant of geopolitical risks such as Middle East tensions and US-China relations, as well as the threat of a global growth slowdown.

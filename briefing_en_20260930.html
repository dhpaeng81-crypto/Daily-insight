Finance Financial Markets

US equity markets showed mixed performance with the Dow Jones Industrial Average slightly down 0.3%, while the Nasdaq edged up 0.2% driven by tech strength. Bond yields eased, with the US 10-year Treasury yield falling 5 basis points to 4.45%, supported by a weaker dollar and cooling inflation expectations. Traditional sectors such as banking, capital goods, and rail services underperformed, in contrast to gains in gold-related and digital asset sectors. Globally, US Treasuries and gold are increasingly attractive compared to emerging market equities, which saw softened inflows. These factors underscore a cautious stance among investors amid geopolitical and macro uncertainties.

Tech AI · Tech Trends

OpenAI is reportedly pursuing a new funding round valuing the company at approximately 1.4 trillion KRW (around $1.4 trillion USD), fueled by advances in AI chip technology and broadening commercialization of AI applications. The company recently unveiled pioneering technologies such as GPT-6.1 and the AI agent 'Dots,' accelerating diversification of AI software platforms. This intensifying AI race is expected to widen technological gaps in Korea’s AI platform, memory semiconductor, and optical communication sectors. With the AI market growing at an annual pace near 20%, major Korean IT conglomerates are well positioned to reap considerable benefits in the latter half of the year.

Energy Energy · Industry

Middle East crude oil exports rose by 6% compared to August, alongside a 4% increase in liquefied natural gas (LNG) shipments through the strategic Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude prices reached $92 per barrel, marking a 5% month-over-month increase. In response to the soaring diesel price, which surpassed $6 per gallon, the US government plans to release an additional 40 million barrels from its strategic petroleum reserve. The UK is advancing power grid upgrade policies aimed at reducing energy costs. Concurrently, diminished Iranian influence is enhancing supply stability in Gulf oil fields.

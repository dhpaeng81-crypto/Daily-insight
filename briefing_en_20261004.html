Finance Financial Markets

Today, major global equity markets rebounded with defensive strength led by technology and AI-related stocks. The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 0.7%, while the Nasdaq Composite gained 1.3%. Safe-haven assets like gold and Bitcoin strengthened amid a weaker U.S. dollar and expectations of a dovish Federal Reserve stance. Industrial and commodity sectors experienced rotation, highlighted by significant contract wins such as Westinghouse Air Brake's $700 million deal and movements in gold mining through asset sales.

Tech AI · Tech Trends

In the AI sector, Anthropic’s $100 million initiative to train 10,000 engineers over two years indicates rapid expansion in the AI talent pool. Revenue growth in AI semiconductors, data centers, and personal AI agents is accelerating at annual rates between 30% and 70%. Leading Korean companies such as Naver and Samsung SDS are investing heavily in cloud and AI services, while global giants including Anthropic, Meta, and Amazon continue expanding their AI infrastructure. However, AI trustworthiness and data ethics controversies, exemplified by OpenAI challenges, remain salient issues shaping the technology landscape.

Energy Energy · Industry

Crude oil prices remain confined in a range near $89 to $91 per barrel amid ongoing geopolitical uncertainty and mixed policy signals. Factors include Iran’s deteriorating oil export capabilities and currency crisis, development of new African oil fields, and sluggish investments in renewable energy infrastructure. Electricity, gas, and refining costs are rising globally, impacting industrial production costs in countries including Korea. Despite energy transition efforts, transmission network shortcomings in Australia and elsewhere highlight persistent bottlenecks in moving toward renewable energy.

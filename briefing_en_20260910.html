Oil Surges Above $100, SK Hynix & Samsung SDI Rally Sharply

US-Iran tensions boost energy and AI sectors; consumer and food stocks remain weak

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Major indices showed mixed results today with the Dow Jones down 0.3% while the tech-heavy Nasdaq rose 0.5%, reflecting strength in technology stocks but weakness in consumer staples and cyclical sectors. South Korea’s semiconductor and secondary battery sectors led gains as SK Hynix and Samsung SDI jumped over 3% driven by strong foreign investor demand. Global energy prices surged amid escalating US-Iran tensions, adding uncertainty to risk assets overall. The macro backdrop remains volatile, prompting cautious trading with selective positioning in growth areas.

💡 Analyst Insights
The semiconductor and secondary battery sectors remain favored by foreign investors, showing robust short-term momentum. SK Hynix climbed following memory chip supply tightness and a target price upgrade to KRW 220,000 by a leading US investment bank. Samsung SDI soared on expectations of energy transition demand and supportive European green policies, while Samsung Electronics also outperformed alongside its peers. On the global front, Regeneron Pharmaceuticals attracted buying interest after beating Q2 earnings estimates. Conversely, food retailers like Kroger continue to face selling pressure due to inflationary headwinds and slowing sales. We recommend increasing exposure to semiconductors and battery stocks in the near term but advise caution due to geopolitical risks such as US-Iran conflict and oil price volatility, which could bring broader market weakness.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sectors surged again, fueled by Apple’s launch of its first foldable iPhone and growing momentum around AI platforms including OpenAI and Apple. The global AI industry is projected to grow by 18% in 2024, underlining strong secular demand. Apple’s introduction of the foldable ‘iPhone Duo’ and AI-enabled watch marks a leap in manufacturing innovation. Korean tech giants SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics gained nearly 7%, benefiting as key suppliers of AI-related hardware components. Despite regulatory and privacy challenges affecting data centers, Korean companies continue to gain from AI hardware and software adoption trends.

Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices (Brent crude) broke above $101 per barrel, marking a year-to-date high, boosted by US strikes on Iranian oil tankers and reciprocal missile threats from Iran. Oil prices jumped 8% over the week amid escalating Middle East tensions. The US Energy Department raised the 2027 diesel price outlook by 33 cents per gallon, signaling rising costs for logistics and petrochemical industries. While renewable energy policies are advancing globally, near-term concerns over oil and gas supply disruptions are weighing on market sentiment.

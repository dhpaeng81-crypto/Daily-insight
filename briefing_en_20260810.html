Berkshire Hathaway’s massive stock purchases and Middle East energy tensions highlight market directional change.

Tech AI · Tech Trends

Global AI data centers are expanding rapidly, with market size expected to grow 25% in 2024 to approximately $50 billion. South Korean firms like SK Hynix and Naver Cloud are scaling up data infrastructure to capitalize on this trend. However, environmental concerns and regulatory scrutiny related to large-scale data center operations are also gaining prominence.

Energy Energy · Industry

Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East—highlighted by Houthi attacks on Saudi oil infrastructure—and disputes over Greenland energy projects have pushed oil prices up 1.2% toward $84 per barrel. Natural gas prices rose 0.7%, supported by new demand expectations from the Eastern Mediterranean, particularly Cyprus. Additionally, extreme weather events in regions like New Zealand and parts of North America have strained power supplies. Europe’s dependence on imported natural gas is projected to increase, with imports starting in 2028, underscoring sustained energy security concerns.

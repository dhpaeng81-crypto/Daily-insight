Bitcoin Soars 150%, AI Investments Surpass $10 Billion Milestone

Bitcoin and AI stocks lead gains amid rising energy market volatility

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global and Korean equity markets continued their upward momentum, driven by strength in technology and alternative assets. The Nasdaq Composite index rose 1.2% yesterday, reflecting robust investor demand for growth-focused stocks. Bitcoin rallied sharply, appreciating approximately 150% since the inauguration day, climbing from $1,000 to $2,500. In contrast, gold's performance was more modest, rising 30% over the same period, indicating a relatively weaker inflow. Semiconductor and AI-related sectors spearheaded market gains, while defensive sectors and consumer staples remained cautious. Meanwhile, market volatility is expected to persist amid U.S. Treasury interventions in Japan’s forex market and ongoing uncertainties in trade and bond markets.

💡 Analyst Insights
The primary market drivers currently are Bitcoin and leading AI stocks, fueled by a convergence of rising alternative asset preferences and expectations for technological innovations. Korean companies such as Samsung Electronics, benefiting from expanded memory and AI semiconductor supply, and Kakao, with its blockchain and virtual asset businesses, are direct beneficiaries of this trend. On the global front, Nvidia continues to dominate the AI semiconductor space with over 80% market share. We recommend increasing exposure to tech and alternative assets in the short term. Nevertheless, investors should implement stop-loss orders and staggered buying strategies, as risks from U.S. intervention in the Japanese yen, interest rate hikes, and geopolitical tensions could trigger abrupt market reversals.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sectors are experiencing unprecedented growth driven by massive investments in cloud infrastructure and AI capabilities. Leading global firms are launching advanced AI models, establishing new data centers, and signing large-scale cloud contracts, exemplified by Anthropic’s $10 billion deal with cloud startup Volta. Nvidia’s leadership, through its Open Secure AI Alliance now comprising over 120 companies, is fueling global AI expansion, projected to grow more than 30% annually, potentially reaching a $350 trillion market by 2030. Within Korea, Samsung Electronics, LG Electronics, and Naver are spearheading developments in super-large AI models that are reshaping the industry landscape.

Energy Energy · Industry

The global energy market exhibits mixed signals as oil prices declined slightly on hopes of easing geopolitical risks around the Strait of Hormuz. Conversely, power and natural gas prices are exhibiting increased volatility, driven by accelerated renewable energy adoption. The UK aims to become Europe’s largest power producer by 2030, while Australia is expanding solar support programs to reduce electricity costs for businesses and farms. Concurrently, hydrogen turbine technologies are entering commercial phases, supporting long-term growth projections for clean energy sources to exceed 40% of overall energy production. Short-term market fluctuations will persist amid ongoing geopolitical uncertainties and accelerating investments in renewable infrastructure.

Energy Energy · Industry

The U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve has dropped to 346.6 million barrels, the lowest level since 1982, reflecting sustained withdrawals. WTI crude oil prices fell 2.14% to $77.52 per barrel, with Brent down 1.9%. Despite geopolitical supply concerns, including a drone strike on a Russian refinery and secured LNG shipments to Pakistan, the market is currently adjusting to improved global supply-demand balances. Renewable energy sectors also experienced weakness in tandem with traditional energy declines.

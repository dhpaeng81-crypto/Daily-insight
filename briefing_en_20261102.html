AI Security Shift and Middle East Risks Push Oil Down 1%

AI sector correction coincides with energy geopolitical tensions; defensive stocks gain focus

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US equities edged lower, with the Nasdaq Composite declining around 0.5%, reflecting intensified pressure on large-cap growth stocks amid peak interest rate expectations and earnings concerns. Defensive dividend-yielding sectors and energy shares outperformed relatively. The Korean Won traded near 1,370 against the US dollar, supported by modest safe-haven demand. Global inflation data showed a slowdown to a 0.2% monthly increase, while benchmark bond yields fell approximately 0.1 percentage points, indicating cautious sentiment.

💡 Analyst Insights
In the near term, defensive sectors such as utilities, dividend-paying stocks, and healthcare are expected to outperform. Within Korea, Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) stands out due to its role in managing power generation costs amid supply uncertainties, while SK Telecom offers stable telecommunications services with an expanding dividend policy. On the global front, Johnson & Johnson remains a favored defensive play as technology stocks face challenges. We advise increasing defensive stock exposure in portfolios short term. However, investors should monitor risks such as a US economic slowdown materially affecting corporate earnings, which could accelerate broad market declines.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI market is encountering a pivotal moment marked by challenges in large-scale data center environments, a surge in cybersecurity demand, and signs of decelerating technological growth momentum. The global cybersecurity software market is projected to expand 12% year-over-year, reaching approximately $195 billion (around 260 trillion KRW) in 2023. Simultaneously, major technology names are under pressure, driving a rotation in market leadership. In Korea, this has resulted in declines among big tech companies like Naver and Kakao as well as data center-related stocks, while firms specializing in security solutions and enhanced IT workforce are positioned to benefit.

💡 Analyst Insights
Cybersecurity enhancement and data center operational efficiency are emerging as key AI-driven growth areas. Korean companies such as AhnLab, which is expanding cloud and AI-based security solutions, and SK Shieldus, focused on security infrastructure tied to data centers, are expected to see earnings growth. Globally, Palo Alto Networks exemplifies a leading beneficiary among cybersecurity providers. We recommend a medium-term overweight position in these beneficiaries. Short-term risks include ongoing sharp declines in technology stocks and heightened regulatory scrutiny on data centers in the US and Europe.
Energy Energy · Industry

West Texas Intermediate crude prices fell about 1% to around $77 per barrel amid mixed signals from easing tensions related to Iran and persistent Middle East geopolitical risks. The recent attack on a Qatari LNG vessel and supply disruptions in emerging oil producers like Algeria and Argentina have amplified short-term volatility. While global energy supply-demand fundamentals are expected to slightly improve in H2 2023, geopolitical uncertainty tied to US elections and European market dynamics continues to weigh. Investment interest in renewable energy transitions remains strong, especially in the US and Middle East regions.

The short-term oil price dip supports margin improvements in refining and chemical sectors but raises cost management challenges for state-owned enterprises and sectors like aviation.

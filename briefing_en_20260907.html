US Inflation, Iran Tensions Drive Stocks and Oil Both Higher

Inflation and Middle East volatility cause short-term gains in finance and energy sectors; AI infrastructure investment grows

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Dow Jones futures rose by 0.3% amid heightened geopolitical risk related to Iran and ahead of US consumer price index release. Positive earnings expectations from tech giants such as Apple and Nvidia bolstered investor sentiment despite restructuring risks highlighted by a 39-year-old ammunition manufacturer filing for bankruptcy protection. Precision medicine attracted capital with McKesson's $2.25 billion additional investment, while software sector gains were led by Salesforce’s improved AI-driven results. South Korea’s Kospi index also closed up 0.5%, helped by foreign buying in semiconductor, secondary battery, and biotechnology stocks.

💡 Analyst Insights
Market optimism persists on expectations that US inflation has peaked and strong performance from large technology firms. Key Korean beneficiaries include SK Hynix, which is rebounding on increased server DRAM demand linked to Nvidia and Micron’s growth, and Samsung Biologics benefiting from global precision medicine expansion and McKesson’s investment partnerships. On the global stage, Nvidia remains a prime pick given its leadership in AI and graphics processors, having surged over 25% in the past three months. We recommend a short-term (within one month) buy and a medium-term (six months) portfolio overweight in these sectors. However, investors should stay alert to downside risks from potential bankruptcies in US small manufacturing firms and escalating geopolitical tensions involving Iran and Russia, which could trigger emerging market sell-offs and currency volatility.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Demand for AI infrastructure is surging, with data center investments expected to increase over 30% this year. Legal challenges against Microsoft and OpenAI by media companies over AI training data highlight mounting concerns about copyright regulations and rising compliance costs. Salesforce's AI segment showed renewed strength with a 6% rebound in share price following improved earnings. In Korea, companies like Naver Cloud and SK Square are expanding their data infrastructure while actively managing copyright risks, which are becoming crucial determinants of corporate valuations.

💡 Analyst Insights
Industries focused on AI data centers and cloud infrastructure—spanning semiconductors to network hardware—are poised for robust growth both domestically and globally. In Korea, SK Hynix is well positioned to expand supply of high-bandwidth memory critical for AI workloads, while Naver is scaling its cloud business aggressively. Nvidia retains a dominant global role with over 80% market share in data center GPUs. We advise a 3-month buy with allocation increases for AI-related equities but caution investors regarding potential copyright litigation from OpenAI's partnerships and the risk of an AI bubble burst that could depress component prices and investor sentiment.
Energy Energy · Industry

The protracted six-month Iran conflict and US strikes on Iranian oil vessels have pushed Dubai crude prices up 2.1% to $86.3 per barrel, with domestic gasoline prices rising to 1,740 KRW per liter. The US is pursuing supply diversification by expanding crude trade with Venezuela and Russia. The US Energy Secretary noted that approximately 30% of global oil shipments transit the Strait of Hormuz, signaling ongoing short-term risks of supply disruption. Although renewable energy policy discussions remain limited, South Korean government interest continues to grow steadily.

💡 Analyst Insights
Rising oil prices strengthen pricing power for Korean refiners like S-Oil and Korean Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering, which benefits from increased demand for new tankers and offshore platforms. Conversely, sectors such as aviation, represented by Korean Air, face margin pressures from escalating fuel costs, while petrochemical firms like Lotte Chemical may endure squeezed profits. Globally, ExxonMobil stands to gain from Iran-related market dislocations. We recommend buying oil-linked Korean stocks like S-Oil and Korea Shipbuilding while advising a cautious stance or reduced exposure in airlines and petrochemicals due to margin risk. Watch for easing of Middle East supply tensions, additional US strategic reserve releases, or global demand slowdown as potential downside risks.

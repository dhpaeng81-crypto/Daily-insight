Nvidia Surges 2.3% Amid Bank of America Buy, Venezuela’s 65 Billion Barrel Oil Shift

US tech and energy sectors rally as AI advancements and Venezuelan oil control reshape markets

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US equity markets climbed driven by strength in large-cap technology stocks. Nvidia led gains with a 2.3% rise following Bank of America’s upgraded buy rating and strong inflows. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index similarly jumped 1.8%, reflecting sector momentum. Financials showed short-term strength due to Deutsche Bank’s €1.5 billion share buyback and improved dividends, yet concerns remain about the long-term impact of peak global interest rates. The 10-year US Treasury yield inched up to 4.27%, underpinning risk appetite, while European stocks ended flat amid energy concerns and FX volatility.

💡 Analyst Insights
Semiconductors and financial sectors are key beneficiaries this period. South Korea’s SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics stand to gain directly from expanded US AI and data center investments, prompting upward revisions in their Q3 earnings forecasts. Globally, Nvidia’s firm hold in AI chips is validated by Bank of America’s positive outlook, with potential upside exceeding 10% in the near term. We recommend increasing portfolio weightings in these sectors tactically, but caution that rapid changes in US rate policy, AI investment overheating, and geopolitical tensions (especially US-China) necessitate limiting exposure to around 30%.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI chip market is experiencing heightened competition fueled by expanding private debt financing, exemplified by Neocloud Lambda’s recent $1 billion loan to acquire Nvidia chips for lease to Microsoft. This underscores the capital-intensive nature of AI infrastructure build-out. Meanwhile, leading AI firms such as OpenAI and Anthropic intensify efforts to enhance model complexity and grow open-source ecosystems. The global AI market is projected to grow more than 30% in 2024, reaching approximately KRW 500 trillion (USD ~420 billion), driving sustained high growth for related Korean industries.

Neocloud Lambda secured $1 billion in private debt financing to purchase Nvidia AI chips, which it plans to lease to Microsoft. Korean semiconductor and IT service companies, including SK Hynix and Hyundai AutoEver, are likely beneficiaries via supply chain integration. Investors should monitor funding costs and potential AI investment bubbles as short-term risk factors.

Trump Announces US Control Over Venezuela’s 65 Billion Barrels of Oil Reserves
Former President Trump declared the US will secure majority control of Venezuela’s proven 65 billion barrel oil reserves, fueling a 2.5% rally in WTI crude to $84 per barrel. This move, combined with easing sanctions and regional opposition to offshore drilling, suggests a supply chain reorganization in global oil markets. Korean refiners such as S-Oil and SK Innovation stand to benefit from higher margins and export opportunities. Investors should keep an eye on Venezuelan political risks and US foreign policy developments impacting oil supplies.

SK Hynix is poised for a triple-digit operating profit surge in Q3 driven by increased supply share of high-bandwidth memory (HBM). Energy Energy · Industry

Former US President Trump's announcement of securing majority control over Venezuela’s vast 65 billion barrels of proven oil reserves pushed WTI crude prices up 2.5% to $84 per barrel. This development, alongside moves to ease sanctions on Venezuelan oil and state-level opposition to offshore drilling in California, signals a significant reshaping of global energy supply chains. The result is expected to heighten price volatility in power markets, especially among fossil fuel-importing countries.

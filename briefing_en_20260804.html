ETF purchases and rising energy prices highlight timing for asset allocation increase.

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global equity markets saw a clear rotation from technology growth stocks to defensive dividend stocks today. The S&P 500 declined 0.7% and the Nasdaq dropped 1.1%, while dividend-focused ETFs like the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF gained 0.2%. Profit-taking hit tech giants such as Tesla, alongside a 7% surge in short-term U.S. Treasury demand in two weeks, reflecting increased safe-haven buying amid mounting economic uncertainty. Global interest rates rose by 0.05 percentage points, while the U.S. dollar strengthened significantly, prompting capital outflows from emerging markets.

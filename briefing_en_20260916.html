AI-driven data center growth triggers sharp rises in energy and gas consumption globally

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global indices closed largely flat, fluctuating within a 0.3% to 0.6% range, amid mixed signals from technology and consumer goods sectors. Consumer discretionary weakness was driven by news of a US specialty apparel chain shutdown and Costco's increased Kirkland motor oil prices to $57.99, signaling consumption slowdown. Conversely, the AI and data center-linked energy sectors showed relative strength, buoyed by ongoing infrastructure investments. Crypto asset XRP showed promising short-term breakout potential above $1.40, suggesting possible upside to $2.10. Overall, the market reflects cautious investor sentiment balancing growth tech against consumer demand concerns.

💡 Analyst Insights
Consumer stocks faced downward pressure due to slower spending and rising input costs. In Korea, asset-backed firms like Emart and Ecopro stood out defensively amid consumption weakness. Logistics and distribution players such as LX International and CJ Logistics are positioned to benefit from cost pass-through pricing linked to higher logistics expenses, similar to Costco’s price hikes. Globally, Walmart differentiates through its cost management and pricing strategies, potentially outperforming peers. We advise reducing exposure to consumer discretionary and retail stocks in the near term while increasing allocations to logistics and asset-heavy sectors. Key risks include a renewed inflation surge triggering interest rate hikes, supply chain disruptions, and sudden drops in consumer confidence.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Explosive growth in AI and data centers has raised significant concerns about energy consumption, especially natural gas in the US, where demand could exceed the combined consumption of Germany and Japan by 2035, estimated at over 200 billion cubic meters annually. Automation of repetitive tasks through AI solutions is accelerating adoption among major technology firms including Meta, FedEx, and cloud infrastructure providers. However, increasing numbers of AI project failures even among leading tech companies are creating a polarized investment landscape. This trend extends opportunities and challenges to Korean IT sectors, requiring selective investment in proven technology and infrastructure firms.

Korean companies such as SK Hynix, specializing in advanced AI semiconductors and memory, and Hyosung Heavy Industries, a leader in power infrastructure and transformers, are poised for near- to mid-term growth. Energy Energy · Industry

Natural gas demand from US data centers is projected to surge sharply, contributing to a 1.7% rise in crude oil prices today, influenced also by a temporary outage in a Saudi Arabian oil pipeline. Meanwhile, Australia’s reliance on gas-fired generation during evening peaks has decreased by nearly 70% due to rapid battery storage installations. Geopolitical tensions, notably energy infrastructure attacks linked to the Russia-Ukraine conflict, continue to add volatility to global energy markets. Energy policy shifts such as public utility discount programs help mitigate consumer cost pressures despite rising wholesale prices.

NAND, AI, and Defense Stocks Rally; Oil Edges Up to $82.9

Strong Q3 outlook for chips and defense amid modest rise in energy prices

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

U.S. markets saw gains led by semiconductor and AI sectors, with the Dow rising about 0.4% and Nasdaq by 0.8%. Semiconductor and AI stocks surged 1.2%, while defense stocks gained 0.7%. Consumer-discretionary sectors like retail and dining lagged due to rising input costs, down 0.3%. Digital service firms tied to digital transformation also faced pressure. Globally, the Fed's rate-hold stance supports a rebound in commodity and energy prices, stabilizing producer currencies.

💡 Analyst Insights
AI and semiconductor sectors continue to exhibit favorable momentum. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are benefiting as NAND memory price declines stabilize and AI server memory demand rises, projecting a 10-20% improvement in Q3 operating profits. Defense stocks including Hanwha Aerospace and LIG Nex1 are poised for roughly 15% sales growth given increased tactical platform orders. U.S. chip giant Nvidia remains the global AI market leader with resilient demand and expanding investment. We recommend overweight positions in AI, semiconductor, and defense sectors. Risks to monitor include potential Fed rate hikes, U.S. consumer weakness, and geopolitical tensions that may widen sector earnings disparities.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI industry, led by Nvidia, OpenAI, and Apple, is advancing through enhanced large AI models and integration of critical hardware, data, and software. OpenAI recently acquired Glass Imaging, a $300 million smartphone camera tech company, while Apple’s iOS 27 update revamps Siri, expecting a 20% boost in usage. Increasingly active AI ethics and regulation discussions have led Microsoft to announce an AI code of conduct. South Korea’s core strengths lie in AI semiconductor and software sectors, with a domestic AI market projected to grow to KRW 36 trillion by 2026 and global reach expanding to KRW 730 trillion.

Key beneficiaries include Korean cloud, mobile, and AI ecosystem players. Naver, Kakao, and Samsung Electronics are ramping up proprietary AI model development and related hardware supply, positioning for over 10% revenue growth in H2 2024. Energy Energy · Industry

WTI crude oil prices have edged up 2.5% over three sessions to $82.9 per barrel, while international natural gas prices rose 1.2%, marking a mild rebound. Energy price volatility persists due to supply concerns in Northern Ireland, Canada, and some U.S. regions. Canada announced plans to invest over KRW 20 trillion (~$15 billion) in nuclear power infrastructure over the next three weeks to enhance grid reliability. Speculations about a temporary energy supply truce related to the Russia-Ukraine conflict surfaced but remain unconfirmed.

Additional energy price hikes could pressure profitability in high-energy-consuming industries like power generation, chemicals, and materials, while benefiting renewable energy, nuclear power, and energy IT sectors. Korean firms such as Doosan Enerbility (focused on nuclear and green hydrogen) and LS ELECTRIC (power IT and smart grids) stand to gain from North American nuclear and grid investments.

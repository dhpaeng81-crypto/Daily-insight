Surge in AI Project Failures Spurs IT Sector Reevaluation in Korea

Intensified AI market scrutiny demands cautious, selective investment amid growth overheating concerns

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Yesterday, South Korea’s KOSPI index declined 0.8% closing near 2,640 points, reflecting broader tech sector weaknesses. In the U.S., the NASDAQ fell close to 2% intraday, highlighting a global correction in growth and semiconductor stocks. Defensive sectors such as financials and defense showed mild resilience, marking a sector rotation trend. Meanwhile, the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield rose by 7 basis points to 4.28%, with the Korean won weakening to around 1,390 per USD. Investor sentiment is dampened by worries over global economic slowdown and an emerging AI bubble burst.

💡 Analyst Insights
Investors should consider reducing exposure to overextended tech and AI growth stocks. Beneficiaries in Korea include Samsung Electronics, which stands to gain from memory chip demand and capital expenditure expansion, and KB Financial Group, a defensive, high-dividend play favored amid market volatility. Globally, Microsoft remains attractive due to its strong AI service platform and consistent 14% annual revenue growth. In the short term, a cautious 'reduce' stance on growth stocks with selective overweight on value and defensives is advisable. Key risks include further Fed rate hikes and escalating U.S.-China tech conflicts that could impair supply chains and investor risk appetite.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT market is undergoing a critical reassessment as a growing number of startups and projects abruptly shut down or miss expectations, signaling a correction from euphoria experienced just a year ago. Global AI market growth projections remain robust at approximately 37% CAGR for 2024, yet overheating concerns and investment fatigue are curbing enthusiasm. Leading Korean IT conglomerates are accelerating adoption of mature technologies and pivoting business models to stabilize growth. At the same time, caution is mounting over inflated AI valuations amid this sector shakeout.

AI News & Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch
The AI graveyard: a running list of projects and startups that didn’t make it
Multiple high-profile AI projects and startups, including Apple’s delayed Siri AI updates and OpenAI’s problematic ‘super app’ launch, have failed or significantly underperformed recently. This trend highlights a rapid market correction and cooling of AI hype, just one year after peak optimism. The Korean IT industry faces pressure to distinguish viable ventures amid broader investor fatigue and AI bubble concerns. Investors should monitor project viability closely and prioritize companies with proven AI integration and sustainable business models.

💡 Analyst Insights
Only high-quality firms specializing in AI solutions and data center infrastructure are poised to deliver tangible earnings growth. In South Korea, Naver is distinguished by its search-driven AI innovations and cloud service expansion, while SK Hynix's focus on specialized HBM memory products positions it well within the AI hardware supply chain. On the global front, Nvidia dominates with a forecasted 90% surge in semiconductor revenue in 2024 powered by AI demand. Investors are advised to reduce short-term positions and seek attractive entry points at lower valuations for medium-to-long-term holds. Regulatory headwinds, bubble bursts, and intensifying big-tech competition remain key risks.
Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices rose modestly due to reported attacks on Saudi airports and oil pipelines, lifting Brent crude by 1.8% to $86 per barrel. Natural gas spot prices spiked nearly 4% following Indonesia’s reduction of exports to Singapore, tightening regional supply. Meanwhile, Wales is considering policies to prioritize undergrounding renewable energy transmission lines, signaling shifts in European energy infrastructure. Additional oil spills in the Arabian Gulf present dispersed operational risks particularly impacting shipping and petrochemical sectors.

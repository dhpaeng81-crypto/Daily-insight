AI Data Center Boom Spurs Korean Tech and Energy Stocks Amid Nvidia Leadership Shift

Short-term focus on AI data center demand and Nvidia CEO return impacts market dynamics

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The KOSPI climbed 0.6% to approach the 2,800 level, led by strength in semiconductors, secondary battery, and energy sectors. Financial stocks retreated 0.3% amid a slowdown in foreign investor inflows. Market optimism was supported by the US Federal Reserve's decision to hold interest rates steady, expectations of rate cuts in H2 2024, and a 0.5% depreciation of the USD against the Korean won. Globally, tech stocks propelled the Nasdaq higher by 1.2%, while European markets followed suit with the German DAX up 1.0%.

💡 Analyst Insights
The AI data center sector, represented by domestic giants KT and SK Telecom, and the green energy segment exemplified by Hanwha Solutions, showed notable outperformance today. KT announced a 1.5 trillion KRW (approx. $1.2 billion) investment in AI data centers for 2024, while SK Telecom revealed a 1 trillion KRW investment plan, which could drive increased AI cloud service contracts. On the global stage, Microsoft has expanded its cloud infrastructure investment ratio from 15% to 20% to accelerate AI service offerings despite leadership uncertainties at Nvidia. We recommend increasing portfolio weights in AI data center and eco-friendly energy stocks. Key downside risks include Nvidia’s CEO transition uncertainties and potential reinstatement of US rate hikes.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s return has drawn significant attention with White House support, though critics warn that overreliance on Nvidia could weaken US industrial competitiveness. Worldwide AI data center investments are expected to rise 25% year-over-year to reach 130 trillion KRW (around $100 billion) in 2023, benefiting AI service providers and cloud infrastructure firms. Korean companies such as Naver Cloud, KT, and SK Telecom are scaling up data center infrastructure aggressively. However, challenges remain as even leading AI models show limitations in reliability and real-world task handling, indicating that commercial viability will require additional time.

💡 Analyst Insights
Domestic beneficiaries in the data center and AI semiconductor spaces include SK Hynix and Naver Cloud. SK Hynix plans to increase supply of high-bandwidth memory (HBM3) for AI servers in 2024, while Naver Cloud targets a 20% operating profit growth next year backed by public and private AI service expansion. Globally, Microsoft is a key driver of AI data center growth through heavy capital expenditure on cloud infrastructure, sidelining Nvidia’s CEO leadership concerns. Our recommendation is to incrementally overweight AI-related Korean tech stocks, while remaining cautious of short-term volatility driven by Nvidia’s governance issues and AI algorithm reliability doubts.
Energy Energy · Industry

Oil prices surged about 6% to $85 per barrel amid escalating Middle East geopolitical tensions. Côte d'Ivoire has announced ambitious plans to boost daily oil production to 500,000 barrels by 2040, attracting international investment. The European Union’s decision to fully ban Russian LNG imports starting January 2024 has heightened strain on global gas supplies. UK households face energy price increases up to 10% this quarter, driving heightened volatility across power and gas markets.

💡 Analyst Insights
Rising crude oil and natural gas prices are expected to benefit Korean green energy and power companies like Doosan Enerbility and Hanwha Solutions over the medium term. Doosan Enerbility is projected to grow sales by 18% in 2024, backed by overseas nuclear and hydrogen plant orders, while Hanwha Solutions strengthens its competitive position with expanding solar cell and module market shares.

Nvidia Rebounds 5%; AI Ecosystem Volatility Expands

Short-term volatility surges in AI and IT; Korean semiconductor and solution firms gain profit opportunities

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The KOSPI closed down 0.7% at 2,645.5 points, pressured by foreign net selling worth approximately 230 billion KRW. Major caps like Samsung Electronics and Hyundai Motor underperformed. In the US, the Nasdaq edged up 0.3% while the Dow dipped 0.1%, as tech and consumer discretionary sectors showed relative strength. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose slightly to 4.50%, and the dollar maintained a firm tone. Global capital selectively flows into AI, semiconductor, and renewable energy sectors, though overall risk appetite remains cautious.

💡 Analyst Insights
AI and semiconductor sectors remain the primary beneficiaries of recent capital inflows. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are poised to improve operating margins above 10% in 2024, driven by expanding US AI investment demand and memory market recovery. Globally, Nvidia's 5% rebound, following founder Jensen Huang’s return, signals proactive investment despite AI ecosystem uncertainties. Investors should tactically increase their holdings in these sectors with phased buying on dips. However, risks such as heightened AI ecosystem volatility, prolonged US interest rate curve inversion, and policy uncertainties warrant careful monitoring.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI market shows a recovery led by Nvidia, alongside growing concerns around next-gen security focusing on data protection and real-world usage limitations of AI. AI startup valuations have dropped sharply, with Anthropic shares falling 8% post-IPO, intensifying competitive differentiation. The global AI industry is projected to reach approximately 380 trillion KRW (~$310 billion) in 2024, growing at 24% CAGR. Key focus areas include AI data protection, cost efficiency, and dedicated hardware, highlighting opportunities for Korea’s memory, system semiconductor, AI server, and application software companies.

Investors should monitor Nvidia’s strategic developments as a bellwether for AI sector momentum.

💡 Analyst Insights
Investment interest is rising in AI security technologies such as data anonymization and cost reduction methods like low-power AI. Korean beneficiaries include Naver, excelling in generative AI and cloud services, and TmaxSoft, specializing in data protection middleware. Samsung Electronics is expected to deliver steady earnings growth with its specialized AI memory and chips. Internationally, Nvidia remains a leader in graphics chips and AI infrastructure, with Meta also notable for its e-commerce AI applications growing above 15%. Investors should increase mid-term exposure while remaining wary of intensifying AI market competition, startup valuation declines, and regulatory risks.
Energy Energy · Industry

Oil prices surged 3.4% to $84.1 per barrel due to North American export disruptions and environmental issues in Canada and the US. Central banks worldwide anticipate a potential second wave of inflation linked to energy costs around 2027. Although short-term momentum for renewable energy weakens amid US policy delays and slowed wind project approvals, the global energy transition pressure remains robust over the medium to long term.

💡 Analyst Insights
Rising energy prices will temporarily boost earnings in refining and petrochemical sectors, benefiting Korean firms such as S-Oil and SK Innovation. Conversely, sectors sensitive to fuel costs like Korean Air and Hyundai Glovis face margin pressures, suggesting a reduction in short-term holdings. Globally, US-based Chevron and Venezuela-linked Golden Rock are poised to gain from increased oil exports. The recommended strategy is to buy refiners and petrochemical stocks short-term while trimming exposure in transport and aviation. Investors should remain vigilant against pollution risks, shifting renewable energy policies, and geopolitical uncertainties including upcoming US elections and Middle East tensions.

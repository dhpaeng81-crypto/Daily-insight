Today's Briefing

OpenAI valued at $1.25 trillion, accelerating AI capital influx

Big AI and cloud investments gather pace; energy price rebound eyed

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

U.S. equity markets showed resilience with tech-led gains as the Nasdaq climbed 1.6% and the semiconductor sector surged 2.2%. South Korea's Kospi edged up 0.4%, supported by foreign inflows concentrated in Financials and Information Technology sectors. Benchmark sovereign yields across major economies fell about 0.10 percentage points, facilitating a move into risk assets. The Korean won appreciated by 18 won, settling near 1,340, bolstering expectations for increased capital inflow. Growth sentiment strengthened further as ETFs focused on technology and clean energy sectors attracted net inflows of approximately 800 billion KRW over the past week.

💡 Analyst Insights
Growth stocks connected to AI and cloud computing—namely big tech, secondary battery, and semiconductor companies—are driving short-term gains. Samsung Electronics benefits from expanding AI investments and increasing AI foundry market share to 26% globally in the U.S. and Europe. Naver’s cloud business is also gaining traction through hybrid AI-cloud sales expansion. Globally, Nvidia's stock has appreciated 24% over the past month amid rising AI investments worldwide. The recommended strategy is to increase portfolio weighting in these sectors, leveraging the projected $1.5 quadrillion global AI market growth. However, investors should cautiously manage risks from a potential rise in U.S. long-term interest rates due to Fed tightening in Q2 and geopolitical uncertainties such as Middle East oil price volatility, employing dollar-cost averaging and sector diversification.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI sector’s momentum accelerated after OpenAI secured an additional $11 billion funding, valuing the company at approximately $1.25 trillion. Google expanded its AI infrastructure by acquiring a new data center site in Wyoming, while Meta is broadening AI collaboration tools with its new ‘Spaces’ platform. The U.S. midterm election outcomes have spotlighted policy risks associated with data center permits and cloud regulation. In South Korea, AI-semiconductor, cloud infrastructure, and AI security markets are poised for vigorous growth, expecting over 20% revenue increases next year. The global AI market growth rate is forecasted to exceed 18% annually through 2028.

Energy Energy · Industry

U.S. natural gas futures jumped 3.5% amid expectations of higher liquified natural gas (LNG) export volumes, surpassing $2.7 per 10,000 BTU. WTI crude oil rebounded 1% amid escalating Middle East geopolitical tensions and supply disruption concerns, trading near $78 per barrel. Climate anomalies and infrastructure spending in resource-rich countries, particularly the U.S. with accelerated implementation of the Senate Bill 254 infrastructure budget, are fast-tracking renewable energy projects. The convergence of energy transition and rising data center power demand is exemplified by the AI-energy mega partnership between Abu Dhabi’s ADNOC, G42, and OpenAI. These supply-demand trends are set to reshape industrial investments in the second half of the year.

The short-term rebound in energy prices directly benefits electric power, gas, and energy equipment firms. Data center power demand is providing stable revenue streams in the power sector. South Korean companies such as Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO), which is expanding data center electricity supply and large-scale power demand, and SK Innovation, which has strong cost pass-through on LNG and battery materials, stand to gain substantially. Investors should prepare for possible price dips and sector corrections if geopolitical tensions ease or global demand weakens.

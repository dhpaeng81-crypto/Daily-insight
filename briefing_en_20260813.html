Nvidia-Driven AI Investment Spurs Gains in Korean Semiconductor Stocks

AI capital inflows and rising chip demand boost Korea’s IT and energy sectors

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US markets showed mixed performance after Bank of America issued a caution on Nvidia shares; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 1.2%. US real estate investment trusts such as Welltower remained flat amid investor focus on interest rates and AI investment trends. South Korea’s KOSPI fluctuated around the 2,800 level but closed with a 2% gain driven by foreign buying in large cap semiconductor stocks including Samsung Electronics. Currency markets reflected increased volatility with a weaker yen and a slight rebound in US Treasury yields.

💡 Analyst Insights
The semiconductor, secondary battery, and AI-related materials and components sectors are benefiting short-term from increased investment by US Big Tech firms. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are expected to report Q3 2024 operating profits of approximately KRW 11 trillion and KRW 4 trillion respectively, supported by sustained demand from Nvidia and other global AI players, as well as tight supply for HBM memory. Nvidia remains the global AI investment epicenter, while REITs like Welltower serve as defensive portfolio options. Our recommended investment action is to overweight semiconductor stocks, justified by a 15%+ recent rise in memory chip prices and a 30% year-to-date growth in global AI fund inflows. However, investors should monitor risks including a stable or resumed US rate hike cycle and semiconductor supply chain restructuring.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI funding environment is rapidly expanding with Cognition currently pursuing a major financing round valuing it near USD 40 billion. OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings secured USD 2 billion from SoftBank and others, highlighting growing capital for AI enterprise solutions. Demand for AI data center infrastructure has more than doubled in the US and Europe within months. Amazon plans to use Twitch video streams as default training data for AI models, while discussions on AI content regulation, including Anthropic’s watermarking technology, are intensifying. Korean conglomerates are ramping up investments in HBM, AI semiconductors, and cloud businesses.

💡 Analyst Insights
Korean industries in AI hardware, GPUs, data centers, and semiconductor equipment are receiving notable benefits during this technology expansion phase. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix plan to increase capital expenditure by over 20% this year to meet soaring demand for high-bandwidth memory and AI-specific DDR5 DRAM. Naver is capitalizing on cloud-based AI APIs and remote work solutions to capture nascent demand. Globally, Nvidia and Amazon Web Services dominate the AI compute cloud market. Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices have lacked clear direction recently, influenced by a Russian tanker attack in the Black Sea and increased shale oil production in the US. Brent crude has oscillated within a 4% range over the past week, while US natural gas prices rose 2% following projections of record 2026 production. Greenland’s oil development project delays caused a 30% plunge in Greenland Energy’s shares. Furthermore, a significant oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico heightened supply uncertainty. Domestic electricity and gas import prices showed slight increases amid this volatile environment.

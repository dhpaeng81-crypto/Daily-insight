International Oil Approaching $80, Focus on Korean Energy Stocks

Rising oil prices and energy infrastructure expansion highlight IT and AI investment gaps

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US 10-year Treasury yields declined by 4 basis points to 4.18% amid increased Treasury bond buybacks. The Nasdaq rose 0.6% while the S&P 500 advanced 0.3%, supported by inflows into IT and energy sectors. Capital One shares showed modest moves after Bank of America maintained a neutral rating. U.S. deposit rates surged to a high of 4.35%, rekindling interest in short-term bonds and deposits. Volatility remains elevated due to fiscal policy uncertainties and liquidity dynamics in the bond market.

💡 Analyst Insights
With short-term yields climbing to 4.35%, capital is moving towards safer assets globally and in Korea. Domestically, major banks such as KB Financial Group and Shinhan Financial Group stand to benefit from wider net interest margins and rising deposit bases. Internationally, Bank of America’s defensible market share and capital ratio improvements make it a stable pick. We recommend a cautious stance with a strategic overweight in IT and energy sectors, complemented by phased accumulation of Korean bank stocks and term deposits. Investors should remain vigilant for any escalation in US fiscal risks or sudden market shifts that could trigger a sharp equity selloff.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

Significant capital continues to flow into AI and cloud computing sectors. Alibaba recently committed approximately $14 billion (18 trillion KRW) to AI investments despite a 12% drop in Q2 net profit. Google announced enhanced features allowing users to designate preferred news sources to mitigate AI-driven declines in publisher traffic. In South Korea, demand for AI-dedicated data centers and data management is expanding, driving growth in the IT infrastructure market. However, rising local opposition in the US, where 75% now oppose new data center developments, presents a regulatory challenge.

💡 Analyst Insights
Korean IT firms like SK Hynix, a leader in memory semiconductors, and Samsung SDS, specializing in data centers and AI services, are positioned for sustainable long-term gains amid growing AI infrastructure demand. Globally, Nvidia remains pivotal for AI chip technology and benefits directly from surging investments. We advise increasing exposure to large-cap Korean IT stocks and selectively accumulating AI infrastructure and semiconductor companies. Nevertheless, investors must monitor potential earnings slowdowns in global IT firms and escalating regulatory scrutiny in the US and Europe, including antitrust concerns and data center constraints, to time entries effectively.
Energy Energy · Industry

As of August 20, 2026, Brent crude oil prices reached $79.8 per barrel, marking a 3.5% rise over the past week. Contributing factors include the resumption of an oil pipeline in California, refining capacity shortages, and surging electricity demand. While renewables such as solar and wind gain ground in the US, increased power consumption by data centers has simultaneously driven demand for natural gas-fired power plants. Korean power generation and refining companies are expected to reflect positive earnings momentum in the near term as energy prices firm.

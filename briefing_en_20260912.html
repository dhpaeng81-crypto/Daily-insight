Oil Nears $100, Samsung Engineering Stock Rises Amid Energy Surge

Sustained oil price rally boosts energy and large infrastructure sectors in Korea

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global stock markets showed clear gains today, driven by easing inflation concerns and commodity strength. The US major indices rose an average of 1.2%, supported by falling oil prices and inflation data matching expectations. South Korea’s main KOSPI index gained 0.9%, while the tech-focused KOSDAQ climbed 1.3%. The financial and energy sectors led advances, each rising over 2%. The US dollar edged up modestly by 0.3%, while Treasury yields declined by 0.05 percentage points, easing inflation worries and stimulating capital flows into emerging markets and resource stocks.

💡 Analyst Insights
Increasing allocations to energy and infrastructure stocks remain timely in the short term. Samsung Engineering and SK Innovation are poised to benefit from the global surge in oil prices and expanding overseas project orders, with the energy and heavy industries’ market capitalization in the KOSPI rising from 7% to 8.5% over the past three weeks. Globally, ExxonMobil recently surpassed $120 per share, reflecting similar tailwinds. Investors should adopt a strategy of accumulating energy and infrastructure shares, selectively buy financial stocks, and adopt a more cautious stance on growth stocks for now. However, geopolitical risks such as Saudi oil pipeline attacks could trigger volatile price spikes, so profit-taking levels should be carefully defined for increased holdings.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI industry is facing intense competition over securing high-quality training data. US startup Mecka AI is close to raising $500 million in a Sequoia-led funding round, valuing it at around $500 million, as robotics-related data demand surges. Open-weight AI models, which promote open training approaches, are emerging as a global trend, with US efforts to counter China’s dominance intensifying. Rising AI security concerns, regulatory pressures on algorithms, and data privacy risks have complicated the innovation landscape. Korean IT service providers saw strong gains, with KOSDAQ and KOSPI IT sector indices up 1.8%, fueled by increased cloud investments and growing optimism for AI-related profitability.

Energy Energy · Industry

Global oil prices surged to $98 per barrel for WTI amid attacks on Saudi Arabia’s critical East-West crude oil pipeline and escalating tensions in Russia, Iraq, and Yemen oil-producing regions. The International Energy Agency warned that world oil demand growth could slow to 2% in H2 2024, but short-term supply fears dominate due to geopolitical disruptions. US natural gas and electricity prices also rose about 15% over the past month, with Korea's monthly wholesale power cost up 9% from the previous month. Conversely, renewable energy producers face short-term profitability pressure from rising raw material and equipment procurement costs.

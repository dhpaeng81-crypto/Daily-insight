Energy Energy · Industry

Oil prices surged 7.2% to $83 per barrel following Saudi Arabia's announcement of a direct sale of 100 million barrels to Asian markets via the Strait of Hormuz. The US energy department has pressured domestic refiners to limit diesel exports amid the rising prices. European markets, including the UK, face upward pressure on crude and wholesale power prices, complicating policy responses. Meanwhile, shale oil M&A activity has completed recent deals, with supply chain consolidation underway. Renewable energy sectors have weakened comparatively amid this fossil fuel price rally.

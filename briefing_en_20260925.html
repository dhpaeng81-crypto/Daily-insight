Tech AI · Tech Trends

New AI developments—ranging from AI chips, ultra-compact large language models, to data center innovations—are driving volatility. Major global players such as Meta, Qualcomm, and Oracle are expanding consumer AI products and committing approximately USD 3.5 trillion in data center investments through 2028. Increased adoption of real-time AI services boosts API and AI solution sales among Korean small and medium IT firms by an average of 19%. However, regulatory concerns on AI bias and safety are escalating alongside growth.

Energy Energy · Industry

Crude oil prices rose 6.2% over the past week, reaching approximately USD 94 per barrel, approaching historical highs. This price surge results from the cessation of U.S. strategic petroleum reserve releases combined with production cuts by Saudi Arabia and Russia, reigniting supply concerns. Electricity and gas prices in Europe and Asia have increased by about 12% year-over-year, exacerbating energy cost pressures. Domestic Korean oil refining and secondary battery sectors are rapidly reflecting margin improvements amidst this environment.

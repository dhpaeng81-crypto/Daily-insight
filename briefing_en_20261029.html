Microsoft Azure Quarterly Revenue Hits $33.2 Billion, Revealing AI Cloud Growth

Big tech earnings and AI product launches drive IT and AI sector surge

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

U.S. major indices closed sharply higher, led by strong earnings from large technology companies. The S&P 500 rose 1.1% to 5,535 points, while the Nasdaq advanced 1.7% to 17,950 points. Technology and communication service sectors outperformed, each gaining over 2%, whereas financials and materials saw more modest increases. Global bond yields climbed about 10 basis points, creating some volatility, but expectations of a potential Fed pause limited market shocks.

💡 Analyst Insights
Tech and big tech companies are once again driving market rotation. Microsoft and Alphabet posted quarterly revenues of approximately KRW 92.3 trillion (+19%) and KRW 127.6 trillion (+25%), respectively, underscoring strong earnings momentum. Korean heavyweights Samsung Electronics and Naver are direct beneficiaries of rising AI, cloud, and search advertising demand. Globally, Microsoft leads the rally backed by Azure and OpenAI business performance. We recommend overweighting IT and telecommunications sectors in the near term while maintaining a cautious stance on financials and materials. However, a sharp rise in U.S. long-term yields could pressure growth stock valuations and elevate short-term downside risk.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

AI-driven cloud computing and search advertising continue to propel global IT earnings. Microsoft disclosed Azure’s quarterly revenue at $33.2 billion (approximately KRW 42 trillion), a first-time transparency highlighting robust AI cloud monetization. OpenAI-related revenue contributed about $10 billion alone, reflecting accelerating AI commercialization. Alphabet expanded global AI-powered search ads, raising total revenue by 25%, and revealed competitive benchmarks for its new Gemini 4 Carbon AI model. Domestic semiconductor giants Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix benefit from strong AI semiconductor demand, boosting profitability.

💡 Analyst Insights
Korean IT firms are poised to capture significant AI-driven sales growth from cloud, semiconductors, and advertising. Samsung Electronics is experiencing surging sales of high-bandwidth memory products, while Naver is slated to gain from AI-boosted search advertising revenues. On the global front, Microsoft successfully crossed simultaneous profitability and market share growth in its Azure and OpenAI cloud businesses. We advise increasing exposure to Korean AI and cloud IT companies over the next three months and accumulating positions in mid-term AI and cloud-related stocks. Risks include failure to commercialize AI models and increased antitrust scrutiny of big tech.
Energy Energy · Industry

International oil prices rose about 1% to $83 per barrel despite renewed Middle East geopolitical tensions and announcements to expedite LNG permitting. The U.S. Department of Energy approved approximately KRW 6.7 trillion in new national loans to support nuclear power investments, with regulatory reforms to shorten LNG approval timelines also underway. Investments in renewable energy and hydrogen projects are escalating. Prices in electricity and natural gas markets showed modest short-term gains.

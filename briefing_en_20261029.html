Microsoft Reveals $33.2 Billion Azure Revenue for First Time

Strong AI and cloud earnings from US big tech rekindle growth stock momentum

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

US financial markets advanced as the S&P 500 rose 1.3% to reclaim 5,011 points, driven by positive quarterly results from major tech players. The Nasdaq surged 1.9%, led by Microsoft and Alphabet’s strong earnings displaying resilience in the IT sector. The 10-year US Treasury yield fell 11 basis points to 4.25% following the Federal Reserve's rate hold and dovish comments, boosting investor interest in growth stocks. Semiconductor, IT software, cloud, and financial sectors outperformed, while energy and consumer discretionary faced short-term pullbacks.

💡 Analyst Insights
The equity market rally is anchored by AI- and cloud-driven strength in IT and semiconductor sectors. Domestically, Samsung Electronics benefits from AI and cloud infrastructure momentum, while Naver is strengthening its cloud and AI engine capabilities, setting up short-term earnings catalysts. Globally, Microsoft’s first disclosure of Azure revenue nearing $33.2 billion signals intensifying cloud competition with AWS, alongside Alphabet’s continued expansion in AI-powered search advertising. We recommend increasing exposure to growth stocks, especially Korean large-caps in IT, amid stabilizing currency conditions. However, investors should watch for risks such as a renewed rise in interest rates or disappointing earnings from technology companies.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT markets are invigorated by recent earnings revelations from US tech giants. Microsoft posted $33.2 billion in Azure revenue and $10 billion from OpenAI-related commercial activities this quarter, marking its 10th consecutive record quarter with $92.3 billion total revenue, up 19% year-over-year. Alphabet reported a 25% revenue increase, reaching $127.6 billion annually, despite slowing traditional ad growth, thanks to global rollout of AI-driven search ads. Nvidia shares faced correction amid intensifying competition in AI semiconductor space. Korean IT firms are accelerating cloud and AI service offerings and deepening collaborations with OpenAI and Google to capture this expanding market.

Energy Energy · Industry

Energy markets showed steady to slightly higher crude oil prices around $83 per barrel, while international natural gas prices rebounded by approximately 7% over three months due to LNG terminal expansion news. The US government’s approval of an $8.2 billion LNG deepwater port highlights anticipated global supply growth. European natural gas inventories remain adequate, and renewable energy investments in the UK and Germany are driving power prices toward stabilization. However, localized electricity rate hikes in US and UK households, policy uncertainties, and mixed expectations about economic recovery following Fed rate decisions continue to add volatility.

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hits 4.35%, Venezuela Oil Deal Spurs Energy Volatility

Rising US bond yields and Venezuelan oil output agreement heighten financial and energy sector fluctuations

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The US 10-year Treasury yield surged to 4.35%, the highest level since January 2025, triggering declines across global equities. The Nasdaq Composite fell 1.2%, the S&P 500 decreased by 0.8%, while South Korea's KOSPI closed down 0.6%. Defensive sectors and high-dividend financial stocks outperformed amid growth stock sell-offs. US bank stocks, including Scotiabank (BNS), gained on strong quarterly earnings, whereas consumer and restaurant stocks weakened amid bankruptcy and demand concerns. The forex market showed simultaneous US dollar strength and anticipation of a Japanese yen rebound.

💡 Analyst Insights
The sharp rise in bond yields is pressuring overvalued growth and tech stocks, driving preference toward financials, energy, and high-dividend sectors. In Korea, KB Financial Group and Shinhan Financial Group stand out with stable earnings and attractive dividends, expected to outperform in Q3. Globally, Scotiabank beat its quarterly earnings target, strengthening investor confidence in bank stocks. We recommend increasing exposure to financial and banking shares while reducing or cautiously monitoring growth stocks for the near term. However, should US treasury yields spike further causing capital flight from emerging markets or broader economic slowdown, even defensive financial stocks could face downward pressure; thus, adding defensive equities and liquid assets remains prudent.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI sector continues rapid advancement driven by enhanced models and rising security needs, pushing firm valuations higher. OpenAI introduced a new deep reasoning technique, while Anthropic launched the Claude 5.1 series specialized in coding and knowledge tasks. AI security firm HiddenLayer secured an additional $100 million in funding, highlighting growing enterprise demand. The US government’s official support for using copyrighted data in AI training underpins industry expansion. In Korea, AI data centers and AI security ventures alongside semiconductors are capturing short-term momentum.

Energy Energy · Industry

Global energy markets are balancing increased oil supply from the US-Venezuela agreement against dwindling European natural gas reserves. Venezuela aims to boost oil production to 2 million barrels per day by 2030 following the recent pact. European gas storage levels declined to 60%, down 10 percentage points year-over-year, raising winter supply concerns. Oil prices stabilized around $82 per barrel, with European natural gas (TTF) edging up 3%. Both fossil fuel and renewables sectors face heightened short-term volatility.

OpenAI $1.25T Valuation, Nvidia Boosts AI Investments

Massive AI capital inflows drive growth stocks; energy market faces continued supply imbalance

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

The Korean stock market closed higher with the KOSPI up 1.2%, led by large-cap IT and AI-related stocks. The KOSDAQ index rose 0.8%, propelled by strong performance in semiconductor and internet sectors, while secondary battery and biotech sectors saw slight declines. The US Nasdaq gained 1.5% to a new all-time high, supported by a slight drop in the 10-year US Treasury yield to 4.3%. This easing of bond yields has created a more favorable environment for growth stocks globally.

💡 Analyst Insights
Growth stocks centered on AI and semiconductors are poised for near-term outperformance. In Korea, Samsung Electronics remains a prime beneficiary due to its leadership in memory chips and AI GPUs, while Naver is expanding its generative AI services portfolio. Globally, Nvidia continues to dominate the semiconductor and AI ecosystem space, making it a valuable investment. We maintain a 'buy' stance with an increased allocation to these growth segments. However, risks include weakening US consumer data and tightened US-China semiconductor export controls, suggesting investors reduce exposure to economically sensitive sectors.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

OpenAI is pursuing a record-breaking $30 billion funding round, targeting a valuation above $1.25 trillion, the largest ever for a private AI company. Capital is flowing rapidly into AI convergence projects such as robotics, autonomous driving, and entertainment. Nvidia is expanding investments into robot toys and AI content startups, stimulating growth in Korea’s AI data, semiconductor, and cloud infrastructure sectors. This surge is expected to reshape the job market, driven by more widespread AI adoption across industries.

Energy Energy · Industry

A new annual 4 million-ton LNG contract between Qatar and the US underscores ongoing geopolitical volatility impacting global energy supply. Dubai crude oil prices rebounded 1.5% to $86 per barrel recently amid concerns over supply disruptions, including tanker attacks in the Middle East. Regulatory relaxations on energy standards for new US housing, plus major European urban efficiency projects, are progressing. These developments reflect persistent energy supply-demand imbalances and heightened geopolitical risk.

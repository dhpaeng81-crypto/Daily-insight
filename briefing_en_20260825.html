Gold & Energy Stocks Surge 5-10%, AI Startup Valued at $6B

Safe haven demand lifts gold and energy shares amid geopolitical tensions; AI funding momentum continues

💹 Finance 🤖 AI · Tech ⚡ Energy 📈 Stocks
Finance Financial Markets

Global financial markets favored safe assets, pushing gold to $2,350 per ounce, up 1.2%. Energy and commodity stocks rose in tandem. US equities posted modest gains with the Dow Jones up 0.3% and Nasdaq up 0.5%, while US Treasury yields stabilized near 4.2%. Home insurers improved earnings by delaying claim payouts, generating $88 million daily in investment income. Meanwhile, US political developments, including policies related to former President Trump, heightened volatility in related equities.

💡 Analyst Insights
Gold and major energy names are benefiting from heightened economic uncertainty and geopolitical risks. Korean companies such as Korea Zinc, a global leader in gold refining, and Hanwha, which spans oil refining and defense sectors, are positioned to gain from rising gold prices and energy policy shifts. Globally, Newmont remains a key beneficiary among gold miners. Investors are advised to moderately increase gold and energy exposure, capping allocations around 10%, while insurance stocks also present attractive income opportunities. However, risks from Federal Reserve interest rate moves, political instability particularly around US policy changes, and potential sharp dollar appreciation could trigger market selloffs.
Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI sector is experiencing strong capital inflows with startups and hardware companies advancing rapidly. Notably, General Intuition is raising funds at a $6 billion pre-money valuation as it expands into robotics applications. AMD launched its next-generation AI embedded processors, contributing to accelerated edge AI adoption. Despite rising privacy and security concerns related to AI assistants, major tech firms are intensifying new AI ventures. Korea's semiconductor, IT services, and AI solution providers are among the prime beneficiaries of this growth wave.

Energy Energy · Industry

WTI crude oil prices oscillated between $78 and $84 per barrel over the past month, driven by international conflicts and supply concerns. US energy stocks rallied strongly, with some shares linked to former President Trump surging by over $15 million. Canada invested more than 100 billion KRW (~$1 billion) in renewable natural gas production, although trade tensions with the US have increased uncertainty over cross-border electricity relations. Both renewable and traditional energy sectors are experiencing elevated short-term volatility.

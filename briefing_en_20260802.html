Investor focus is shifting toward large-cap growth stocks and companies with strong cash flows. Korean semiconductor heavyweights Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix continue to attract capital due to solid fundamentals and export demand. Tech AI · Tech Trends

The AI and IT sector is experiencing transient volatility ahead of key earnings reports from major technology firms. AI-focused quant funds have suffered declines exceeding 40% in recent months, reflecting investor uncertainty. Expansion of mid-tier and low-cost data center infrastructure, particularly in Mexico, highlights evolving production and cost dynamics. Meanwhile, social platforms like Reddit face challenges from AI-induced user growth slowdowns and competitive data monetization pressures. Korean IT strategies increasingly prioritize investments in mobility, semiconductors, and data center-linked industries.

Energy Energy · Industry

Oil prices remain pressured upward near $81 per barrel due to heightened geopolitical tension involving Iran and Russia. Potential U.S. airstrikes targeting Middle Eastern energy facilities and Ukraine’s attempts to disrupt Russian energy exports add supply uncertainties. Conversely, new supply sources are emerging, including a one-year Turkey-Iraq oil pipeline agreement and a $26 billion oil field development project in Suriname. The energy sector shows a dual trend with price-driven short-term gains and long-term acceleration in energy efficiency and conservation industries.
